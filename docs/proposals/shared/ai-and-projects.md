## Appendix B. AI strand for Classes 6-8

CBSE's 20 AI hours a year in Classes 6-8 are organised into four chapters per class. CBSE's student books explain the concepts; the teacher books give exercises and answer keys. CodeSchool adds a teacher script and a hands-on activity for every chapter, in an offline form (cards, paper and one projector) and an online form (browser activities with supplied datasets). Schools can use either form.

### Class 6

| CBSE AI chapter | What students learn | Offline activity | Online activity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Introduction to AI and everyday examples | What AI is; AI vs automation; supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning | "Is it AI?" sorting cards; "human classifier" game with labelled pictures | Train a picture sorter with a supplied set of labelled images |
| 2. Basic data concepts | Types of data; collecting and organising data | Class survey on paper; build a frequency table | Enter survey data and see charts update |
| 3. Simple pattern recognition and decision making | Finding patterns; observations vs conclusions | Pattern cards and decision trees on chart paper | Build a decision tree and test it on new examples |
| 4. Ethics and digital responsibility | Passwords, phishing, digital footprint, plagiarism | Scenario cards and class debate | Interactive scenarios with instant feedback |

### Class 7

| CBSE AI chapter | What students learn | Offline activity | Online activity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. AI domains and applications | Data science, computer vision, language; classification, regression, clustering | Card labs: classify fruits by features; group "unlabelled" cards into clusters | Train a classifier, a simple prediction line and a clustering tool on small datasets |
| 2. AI in industries | Case studies of AI in health, transport and farming | Case-study cards and role-play | Explore case studies with short quizzes |
| 3. Data visualisation and analysis | Bar, line and pie charts; reading trends | Hand-drawn charts from class data | Build charts from a dataset and spot trends |
| 4. Ethics and AI bias awareness | How unbalanced data causes unfair results | "Lopsided deck" activity: a biased card set gives unfair answers | Retrain a model after rebalancing its data and compare results |

### Class 8

| CBSE AI chapter | What students learn | Offline activity | Online activity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. AI project lifecycle | Define the problem, collect data, test, reflect and improve | Plan an AI project on a lifecycle worksheet | Run the full lifecycle with a no-code model |
| 2. AI and its applications | No-code AI tools; environment, health and education examples | Teacher-led demonstration of CBSE's waste-classifier lab on one projector | Guided version of CBSE's waste-classifier and voice-command labs, with supplied data |
| 3. Data and fairness in AI | Bias in datasets; rebalancing | Card-based fairness audit | Guided version of CBSE's cat/dog bias lab: test, rebalance, retrain |
| 4. Ethics and responsible AI | Privacy, misinformation, responsible use | Debate and "spot the fake" cards | Interactive misinformation and privacy scenarios |

## Appendix C. Project kits for Classes 6-8

CBSE allots **40 hours a year to two required projects of 20 hours each** in Classes 6-8, assessed by the computer teacher. CBSE has said a project list with rubrics is coming; until it is published, CodeSchool's kits follow the topics CBSE presented in its April 2026 webinar, and will be realigned to CBSE's official list as soon as it appears.

Every kit contains: a student brief, a teacher guide with a week-by-week plan, data sheets or datasets, a worked example, a marking rubric (Appendix D), and a presentation template.

| Class | Project 1 (20 hours) | Project 2 (20 hours) |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | **Weather across states:** collect and compare weather data from different states, find patterns and present conclusions | **A smarter school routine:** design an algorithm and flowchart to solve a school problem (for example, library returns or lunch queues), then test it |
| 7 | **Nutri-Quest weekly food planner:** use nutrition data and rules to design a balanced week of meals | **Sort it with AI:** build a classifier for school waste (cards offline, a trained model online) and measure its accuracy |
| 8 | **Sports participation and performance:** collect sports data, analyse it and recommend changes | **Fair AI audit:** test a classifier for bias, rebalance the data and present recommendations |

### Standard 10-week project plan (about 2 hours a week)

| Weeks | Stage | What students do | Evidence for assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Define | Understand the problem, form teams, plan questions | Problem statement and plan |
| 3-4 | Collect | Gather data or design the first version of the algorithm | Data sheet or first flowchart |
| 5-6 | Build | Organise data, make charts, build the algorithm or model | Charts, flowchart or model |
| 7-8 | Test and improve | Test with new cases, find errors, improve | Test log with changes made |
| 9 | Reflect | Discuss fairness, limits and what was learned | Short reflection |
| 10 | Present | Share findings with the class or at a showcase | Presentation and final product |
