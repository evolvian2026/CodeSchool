## 1. What has to be true on 1 April 2027

| By 1 April 2027 | Why |
|---|---|
| Packs printed and with appointed booksellers in each launch city | Parents buy books in the last week of March and the first week of April |
| A classroom kit and teacher book delivered to every adopting section | Teachers need them in the first CT&AI period |
| Every adopting teacher has had a 2-hour orientation (late March or first half of April) | The programme is teacher-led; untrained teachers will not use it |
| CodeSchool listed on each adopting school's published 2027-28 booklist | Booklists are finalised in December-January |
| Pilot results from January-March 2027 written up | Evidence for schools still deciding, and for fixing content |

Working back, the critical path is: **brand and scope decision → specimen copies by mid-December → booklist adoption in December-January → firm orders by mid-February → print and kits by mid-March → booksellers and orientation in late March**.

> [!warn] **The tightest step is content.** The full Offline Programme is about 1,300 printed pages (six workbooks, six project booklets, six teacher books) plus kit artwork, and it has to be print-ready by 10 February 2027. That is about 12 weeks from today. The scope decision below sets how big that job is.

## 2. Three decisions needed by 20 November 2026

### Decision 1: launch scope

| Option | What launches in April 2027 | Content to finish by 10 Feb | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Full range | Packs for all six classes (3-8), kits, teacher books | About 1,300 pages; needs a team of about 6-8 writers, designers and illustrators | High: quality and deadline both at risk |
| **B. Recommended: core range** | Classes 3-5 practice packs, plus a **Classes 6-8 projects and AI pack** (two project booklets and AI activity cards per class, without the full CT workbook) | About 700 pages | Medium: Classes 6-8 CT workbooks follow in 2028-29 |
| C. Narrow pilot launch | Two classes only (for example Classes 4 and 7) in a few schools | About 350 pages | Low, but little revenue and little evidence in 2027-28 |

Option B puts effort where the research found the biggest gap: CBSE's 40 project hours a year in Classes 6-8 have no published project materials or rubrics. It also keeps the Classes 3-5 pack, which fits inside maths periods and needs no devices. The Classes 6-8 projects pack needs its own price, to be set once printer quotes arrive. One risk: CBSE says its own project list with rubrics is "coming soon". If CBSE publishes it first, the project booklets must be realigned quickly.

### Decision 2: specimen format

Schools decide from specimen copies in December-January, before the full books exist.

| Option | What schools receive in December | Recommended? |
|---|---|---|
| **Specimen booklet** | A 24-32-page booklet per band: full contents list, two complete chapters, one project brief with rubric, one teacher-script page, kit photos | **Yes.** Can be ready by 15 December |
| Full specimen books | Complete workbooks | Not possible by December |

### Decision 3: online programme timing

**Recommended: Offline Programme only in April 2027.** The online platform needs an estimated Rs 20-73 lakh and several months to build, and the interviews have not yet confirmed device access. Start building it only if the interviews support it, and pilot it during 2027-28.

### Also needed before specimens are printed

- **Brand name.** The research recommends a coined brand rather than "CodeSchool" before anything is printed. Decide by 20 November and run a trademark search. File the application by 27 November.
- **ISBNs.** Apply through the ISBN agency now: one ISBN per title and edition.

## 6. Main risks

| Risk | Early warning | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Content not ready by 10 Feb | Specimen chapters late on 4 Dec | Narrow the scope further (Option C), or shift the Classes 6-8 booklets to a July reprint |
| Interviews do not support the plan | Go/no-go check on 18 Dec fails | Keep the pilot, drop the April sales push, and aim for April 2028 |
| Few schools adopt in time | Fewer than about 10 schools by 29 Jan | Print short runs only; focus on pilot schools and next year |
| CBSE publishes its project list or rubrics | CBSE circular | Realign project booklets before 10 Feb |
| Distributors expect school commissions | Raised in meetings | Use appointed booksellers and direct school orders only |
| Printer capacity in February-March | Late quotes or long lead times | Book capacity in November with a deposit |
