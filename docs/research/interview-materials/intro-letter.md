# CodeSchool: request for a conversation

*One page. Print it, or paste it into an email or WhatsApp message to the principal or academic coordinator.*

---

Dear [Principal / Coordinator name],

I'm **Anoop Dobhal**, and I'm researching how schools are teaching **Computational Thinking and AI** in Classes 3-8, including CBSE's new curriculum for 2026-27. Before building anything, I want to understand what schools and teachers actually need.

**I'm requesting short conversations** with up to four people at [School name]:

| Who | Time |
|---|---|
| Principal or owner | 30 minutes |
| Academic or CBSE coordinator | 30-40 minutes |
| A Class 3-5 maths or EVS teacher | 40 minutes, including feedback on one printed sample lesson |
| Computer teacher (if the school has one) | 30 minutes |

These can be in person at a time that suits you, or by phone or video call, between **16 November and 11 December 2026**.

**What I'll ask about:** how CT&AI and computer classes are going this year, what teachers find hard, what equipment is available, and how the school chooses academic resources.

**What this isn't:** a sales visit. I'm not selling anything, and there's no product to buy yet.

**Your privacy:**
- Your school and staff won't be named in anything I share.
- I'll take notes. I'll record audio only if the person agrees.
- I won't talk to, photograph or record any students.
- You can skip any question or stop at any time.

**What you get:** a free summary of what I learn from 15-20 schools about how others are handling CT&AI, sent to you in January 2027.

Thank you for considering this. Please reply to this message or contact me at:

**Phone / WhatsApp:** [your number]
**Email:** [your email]

Anoop Dobhal
