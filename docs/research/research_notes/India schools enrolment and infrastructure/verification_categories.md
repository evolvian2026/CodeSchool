How it was checked:
- Official sites were tried first. Live pages read directly: kvs.gov.in, awesindia.com/aboutus.aspx, nests.tribal.gov.in, saras.cbse.gov.in, cisce.org (affiliation page), aees.gov.in, rashtriyamilitaryschools.edu.in and cambridgeinternational.org.

- The EFA UDISE+ PDFs were converted with `pdftotext` and read row by row (residential schools, KV and JNV counts).
- Every cited URL that would load was opened to check that it says what the report claims.
