# Special categories of schools in India and school board affiliation counts

Research date: 6 November 2026. Figures carry their own dates. Where sources conflict, the conflict is stated.

## Kendriya Vidyalayas (KVS): count, students, teachers, new KVs, PM SHRI, facilities

### Takeaway
The KVS website currently shows 1,366 KVs in India and abroad (3 abroad: Moscow, Tehran, Kathmandu), with 14,07,464 students and 50,456 academic staff. Parliament was told in July 2025 that 1,280 KVs were functioning, with 13,50,518 students in 2024-25. The higher current count reflects new KVs opening under the 85 sanctioned in December 2024. A further 57 were approved in 2025, to open from 2026-27. Enrolment in 2024-25 was at a five-year low.

### Cited Findings
- The KVS homepage stats box shows 1,366 KVs "in India and around the world". Three are abroad (Moscow 1, Tehran 1, Kathmandu 1). It also shows 14,07,464 students, 56,810 staff (50,456 academic, 6,354 non-academic) and 25 regions. The page says "last updated 06.11.2026", but that is the page date and is not stated as the "as on" date of the figures — [KVS official site](https://kvs.gov.in/)
- Lok Sabha, July 2025 (Dharmendra Pradhan): "1,280 KVs are functioning across the country". KV totals by year: 13,87,763 (2020-21), 14,29,434 (2021-22), 14,24,147 (2022-23), 13,89,560 (2023-24), 13,50,518 (2024-25). New admissions fell from 1,95,081 (2020-21) to 1,39,660 (2024-25), a 28.4% drop — [Careers360, 28 Jul 2025](https://news.careers360.com/kv-student-enrollment-drops-28-4-percent-in-last-5-years-2025-26-pm-shri-scheme-approved-funds-dip-sharply-govt-lok-sabha)
- KV breakdown by state, July 2025: Uttar Pradesh 125, Rajasthan 78, Jharkhand 41, Andhra Pradesh 37 — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/kv-student-enrollment-drops-28-4-percent-in-last-5-years-2025-26-pm-shri-scheme-approved-funds-dip-sharply-govt-lok-sabha)
- December 2024: the Cabinet approved 85 new KVs plus expansion of KV Shivamogga, at about Rs 5,872 crore, covering about 82,560 students — [News On AIR, 6 Dec 2024](https://newsonair.gov.in/cabinet-approves-opening-of-85-new-kendriya-vidyalayas); [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/union-cabinet-approves-85-new-kendriya-vidyalayas-28-jnvs-and-expansion-of-kv-shivamogga)
- 2025: the CCEA approved 57 new KVs with an outlay of Rs 5,862.55 crore over 9 years from 2026-27. They are expected to benefit 86,640 students. 7 are sponsored by the Ministry of Home Affairs and 50 by States/UTs. These are to be added to the "existing 1,288" KVs — [BW Education](https://www.bweducation.com/article/centre-clears-57-new-kendriya-vidyalayas-across-17-states-uts-573922); [narendramodi.in](https://www.narendramodi.in/cabinet-approves-57-new-kendriya-vidyalayas-worth-rs-5862-crore-598121)
- PM SHRI funds approved for PM SHRI-selected KVs: Rs 399.93 crore for 2025-26, down from Rs 590.71 crore in 2023-24. PM SHRI funds cover ICT labs, science labs, digital libraries, smart classrooms and similar — [Careers360, Jul 2025](https://news.careers360.com/kv-student-enrollment-drops-28-4-percent-in-last-5-years-2025-26-pm-shri-scheme-approved-funds-dip-sharply-govt-lok-sabha)
- December 2025 Lok Sabha reply: over 1,200 KVs and 600 Navodaya Vidyalayas mapped on the PM Gati Shakti portal — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/1200-kendriya-vidyalaya-kv-600-navodaya-nv-jnv-pm-gati-shakti-map-jayant-chaudhary-digital-audit-admission-enrolment-lok-sabha)
- All KVs are CBSE-affiliated — [Wikipedia: CBSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Board_of_Secondary_Education)

### Inferences
- The count moved from 1,280 (Jul 2025) to 1,288 (cited at the 2025 approval) to 1,366 (KVS site, late 2026). Most likely, many of the 85 KVs sanctioned in December 2024 have started functioning. The 1,366 figure may count sanctioned-and-opened KVs, not only fully functional ones.
- Enrolment rose back to about 14.07 lakh on the KVS site. This is consistent with new KVs adding seats, though the reference date of that figure is unknown.

### Gaps
- No national count found of PM SHRI KVs. Most or all KVs are understood to be in PM SHRI, but that was not verified.
- No KVS-wide figures found for computer labs or smart classrooms.
- Admission route not verified this session. KVs are known to give priority to children of transferable central government employees, and Class 1 admission is by lottery.

## Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas (NVS)

### Takeaway
About 653 JNVs are functional in 27 states and 8 UTs, against 661 sanctioned; Tamil Nadu has not adopted the scheme. The Cabinet approved 28 more in December 2024. They are fully government-funded, co-educational residential schools for Classes 6-12, admitting up to 80 students per school in Class 6 through the JNV Selection Test (JNVST). Enrolment is roughly 2.9-3.1 lakh.

### Cited Findings
- July 2024 Lok Sabha reply: "1253 KVs and 653 JNVs are functional". The JNVST 2025 prospectus: 653 functional in 27 States and 8 UTs, "except state of Tamil Nadu" — [Lok Sabha via eparlib](https://eparlib.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2978094/1/AU1027_kNqS5s.pdf); [Wikipedia: JNV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawahar_Navodaya_Vidyalaya)
- 661 JNVs sanctioned, about 653 functioning. The Cabinet approved 28 new JNVs in uncovered districts in December 2024 — [narendramodi.in](https://narendramodi.in/cabinet-approves-setting-up-of-28-new-navodaya-vidyalayas-in-the-uncovered-districts-of-the-country-588785); [News On AIR](https://newsonair.gov.in/govt-approves-85-new-kendriya-vidyalayas-28-navodaya-vidyalayas-nationwide)
- Enrolment: 2.72 lakh (2019-20) rising to 2.88 lakh (2023-24) per a Lok Sabha reply. Wikipedia gives 3,10,517 as of 30 September 2025 — [Lok Sabha reply via eparlib](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/709342/1/7579.pdf); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawahar_Navodaya_Vidyalaya)
- Model: "co-educational residential schools fully financed and administered by Government of India" through the autonomous Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti — [Wikipedia: JNV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawahar_Navodaya_Vidyalaya)
- Admission: at most 80 students per JNV in Class 6 via the JNVST. The Class 6 JNVST for 2027 is scheduled for 28 November 2026. Lateral entry to Classes 9 and 11 fills vacant seats (example notification for 2026-27) — [Careers360 School](https://school.careers360.com/articles/how-many-students-are-selected-in-navodaya); [Nabarangpur district notice](https://nabarangpur.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-11/603-Admission%20Notification%20for%20Class-IX%20%26%20XI%20for%20the%20Session%202026-27%20against%20vacant%20Seats%20in%20Jawahar%20Novadaya%20Vidyalayas.pdf)
- PM SHRI: the government said 620 JNVs will be developed under PM SHRI (headline) — [News On AIR](https://newsonair.gov.in/govt-will-develop-620-jawahar-navodaya-vidyalayas-under-pm-shri-scheme-education-ministry)
- Staffing, December 2025: NVS reported 4,247 teaching and 1,666 non-teaching vacancies — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/navodaya-vidyalaya-samiti-nvs-schools-vacancies-teaching-contractual-appointments-reservation-education-ministry-lok-sabha-reply)
- All JNVs are CBSE-affiliated — [Wikipedia: CBSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Board_of_Secondary_Education)

### Inferences
- With 661 + 28 sanctioned, the system will approach about 689 JNVs once all are set up. The functional count was about 653-654 as of the latest documents found.

### Gaps
- navodaya.gov.in returned no readable content. A 2026 NVS document (cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in, May 2026) was a scanned PDF and could not be read, so there is no official 2026 count of functional JNVs or students.
- The 28 new JNVs' opening status in 2025-26 and 2026-27 was not found.

## Sainik Schools: the 33 original schools and the new partnership-mode schools

### Takeaway
There are 33 "erstwhile pattern" Sainik Schools under the Sainik Schools Society (Ministry of Defence). Against a target of 100 partnership-mode New Sainik Schools (NSS), the MoD had approved 86 by June 2025. For AISSEE 2026, about 69 NSS offered Class 6 admission and 19 offered Class 9. NSS may be residential or day-boarding.

### Cited Findings
- The 2021 scheme invited NGOs, private schools and States to set up 100 new Sainik Schools. Support is fee assistance of 50% of fees, capped at Rs 40,000 a year, for 50% of class strength (up to 50 students), Classes 6-12, on merit-cum-means. There is no government funding for infrastructure — [PIB, 2021](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1706594&reg=48&lang=2)
- March 2022: the MoD approved 21 new Sainik Schools in partnership mode from 2022-23 — [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=1809987&reg=48&lang=2)
- September 2023: 23 more approved, bringing the tally of new partnership-mode schools under the Sainik Schools Society to 42, "apart from the existing 33" Sainik Schools — [PIB, Sep 2023](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=1957912); [News On AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/raksha-mantri-rajnath-singh-approves-23-new-sainik-schools-in-partnership-mode-fulfilling-pms-vision-of-setting-up-100-such-schools)
- June 2025: the MoD has approved 86 new Sainik Schools across three selection rounds, toward the target of 100. New schools begin from Class 6 — [Careers360, 24 Jun 2025](https://news.careers360.com/defence-ministry-approves-86-sainik-schools-under-public-private-partnership-model/amp)
- The 33 original schools are in 24 states/UTs (2022) — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/four-new-sainik-schools-in-partnership-with-civilian-organisations-approved-for-region-477209/)
- AISSEE 2026 (NTA): 3 more NSS added in October 2025. Two are residential (Namakkal, Tamil Nadu; Vasco, Goa) and one is day-boarding (Ambajogai, Maharashtra). At least 40% of NSS seats are filled from the AISSEE all-India merit list (domicile and category not considered), and up to 60% go to the partner school's existing students — [Careers360, 26 Oct 2025](https://news.careers360.com/aissee-2026-nta-adds-3-new-sainik-schools-registration-underway-application-fee-admission-route)
- AISSEE 2026 covered Class 6 admission in 69 New Sainik Schools and Class 9 in 19. The exam was held on 18 January 2026 — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/aissee-2026-sainik-school-registration-begins-for-class-6-9-admissions-apply-at-exams-nta-ac-in) (search snippet)
- An aggregator listing gives 13,932 AISSEE 2026 seats: Class 6 12,527 (3,110 in old schools, 9,417 in new) and Class 9 1,405 (534 old, 871 new). This is unofficial, and a different snippet gave a 71-school basis, so treat it cautiously — [sarkariresultforum](https://sarkariresultforum.com/2025/sainik-schools-entrance-examination-2026)

### Inferences
- The approval sequence was 21 (Mar 2022), then 7 (Aug 2022, per Careers360), then 23 (Sep 2023), then 86 cumulative (Jun 2025). PIB gives 42 rather than 51 after the third batch, which suggests some early approvals lapsed or were not counted as functioning. The functional NSS count in 2026 is likely about 70-86, judging by the 69 schools offering Class 6 seats.
- The original Sainik Schools are fully residential, CBSE-affiliated, Classes 6-12, with girls admitted since 2021-22 (widely reported). This was not re-verified with a primary source this session.

### Gaps
- No official 2025-26 or 2026 count of functioning NSS, or total Sainik School enrolment.
- No official seat matrix (NTA bulletin) was fetched.

## Army Public Schools (AWES) and other defence and central-organisation schools

### Takeaway
AWES runs about 137 CBSE-affiliated Army Public Schools and about 250 Army pre-primary schools; a dated figure puts students at about 2.4 lakh. There are 5 Rashtriya Military Schools (MoD, CBSE). The Atomic Energy Education Society runs about 30-31 schools and colleges with roughly 25,000 students. No reliable current counts were found for Air Force schools, Navy Children Schools, railway schools or Central Tibetan Schools.

### Cited Findings
- 137 Army Public Schools in cantonments and military stations, all CBSE-affiliated and following NCERT. About 250 Army pre-primary schools. About 2.4 lakh students and about 8,500 teachers (older figure, undated) — [Wikipedia: AWES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_Welfare_Education_Society); [ESM Corner](https://esmcorner.com/army-welfare-education-society-awes/); [Wikipedia: Indian Army Public Schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Army_Public_Schools)
- Rashtriya Military Schools: five, at Chail (HP), Ajmer and Dholpur (Rajasthan), and Belgaum and Bengaluru (Karnataka). They are run by the Ministry of Defence and affiliated to CBSE. Admission is through a Common Entrance Test (prospectus) — [ESM Corner](https://esmcorner.com/rashtriya-military-schools-rms/); [RMS CET prospectus 2024](https://apply-delhi.nielit.gov.in/PDF/CET/Rectified_ProspectusRMSs21082024.pdf); [RMS Chail](https://www.rashtriyamilitaryschools.edu.in/rmschail/about.php)
- Atomic Energy Education Society: "25k+ Active Students, 30 Schools & Colleges", affiliated to CBSE and some state boards, serving children of Department of Atomic Energy employees. Wikipedia (citing a budget document) gives 31 schools and junior colleges at 14 locations with more than 26,000 students — [AEES About](https://aees.gov.in/about-aees.html); [Wikipedia: AEES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_Energy_Education_Society)
- Central Tibetan Schools: 14 CTSA schools affiliated to CBSE, in an older CBSE category breakdown — [Wikipedia: CBSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Board_of_Secondary_Education)
- Air Force schools are run by the IAF Educational and Cultural Society; no national count found — [Wikipedia: Air Force School Coimbatore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_School,_Coimbatore)

### Inferences
- All of these networks are, in practice, CBSE-affiliated day schools (except the RMS, which are residential). Most of their students are wards of defence or central employees.

### Gaps
- No reliable current counts found for Navy Children Schools, Air Force schools, Indian Railways schools, or Central Tibetan Schools (students and current school count).
- No current AWES enrolment figure; the 2.4 lakh figure is dated.

## Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS)

### Takeaway
About 728 EMRS were approved as of 2025, of which about 477-479 were functional by mid-2025, with about 1.38 lakh tribal students. A December 2025 report headline says 440 functional, which conflicts with this.

### Cited Findings
- August 2025 Lok Sabha (Jual Oram): 728 EMRS approved in total, 479 functional as of June 2025, with 1.38 lakh students in those 479 schools — [The Hawk](https://www.thehawk.in/news/india/138-lakh-tribal-students-enrolled-in-479-eklavya-residential-schools-minister-jual-oram)
- December 2025: 728 EMRS sanctioned. The headline says "440 EMRS functional across country" — [News On AIR, 18 Dec 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/440-eklavya-model-residential-schools-functional-across-country-tribal-affairs-minister)
- Parliamentary Standing Committee (late 2025): only 477 of 722 sanctioned EMRS are functional (about 58%). It recommended no new sanctions until land is secured — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/eklavya-schools-emrs-scheme-functioning-parliament-panel-tribal-affairs-ministry/amp)
- Example of the gap: 35 EMRS sanctioned for Meghalaya, none functional yet — [Highland Post](https://highlandpost.com/35-eklavya-schools-sanctioned-for-mlaya-none-functional-yet/)
- April 2025: the Centre is setting up 728 EMRS for tribal students — [News On AIR, 2 Apr 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/centre-to-set-up-728-eklavya-model-residential-school-for-tribal-students/)

### Inferences
- The "440" figure probably confuses the 440 new EMRS proposed after 2018-19 with the number functional. The 477-479 figure is better supported.

### Gaps
- Seat capacity, classes and CBSE affiliation were not verified this session. Commonly reported: Classes 6-12, 480 seats per school, CBSE-affiliated, run by NESTS.
- No 2026 functional count found.

## Other residential and boarding schools (KGBV, state residential societies, private boarding, UDISE+ total)

### Takeaway
UDISE+ counts 47,388 residential schools in 2025-26, up from 46,517 in 2024-25. KGBVs (girls' residential schools under Samagra Shiksha) number 5,639 sanctioned, 5,269 functional, with about 7.58 lakh girls (December 2025). State welfare residential societies are large; Telangana's SC society alone runs 268 institutions with about 1.45 lakh students.

### Cited Findings
- UDISE+ 2025-26: residential schools rose from 46,517 (2024-25) to 47,388 (2025-26), up 1.9% — [Arun C. Mehta analysis of UDISE+ 2025-26, Jul 2026](https://educationforallinindia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Has-Indias-School-enrolment-bottom-out-UDISEPlus-2025-26-aruncmehta.pdf); the 2024-25 figure of 46,517 is in the [UDISE+ 2024-25 booklet](https://dashboard.udiseplus.gov.in/report2026/static/media/UDISE+2024_25_Booklet_existing.118ba29d4773e6372f72.pdf)
- KGBVs: 5,639 in total, 370 still non-functional as of 5 December 2025, so 5,269 functional. About 7.58 lakh girls enrolled: 2.64 lakh OBC, 2.06 lakh SC, 1.77 lakh ST. Only about 50% of higher-class students are retained — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/kasturba-gandhi-balika-vidyalaya-kgbv-retention-rate-50-pc-enrolment-education-ministry-sarva-shiksha-abhiyan-class-6-12-school/amp)
- February 2026, Lok Sabha: over 7 lakh girls currently enrolled in KGBVs — [News On AIR, 2 Feb 2026](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/govt-says-over-7-lakh-girls-are-currently-enrolled-in-kasturba-gandhi-balika-vidyalaya)
- Historic comparison (pre-Samagra, about 2018): 3,703 KGBVs sanctioned, 3,697 operational — [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?relid=181706&reg=48&lang=2)
- Telangana: TGSWREIS (SC welfare) runs 268 residential institutions from Class 5 to undergraduate level, 175 of them for girls, with about 1,45,485 students. TGTWREIS (tribal) runs 165 institutions. TMREIS (minorities) runs 205 residential schools — [TGSWREIS](https://tgswreis.telangana.gov.in/); [TGTWREIS](https://tgtwreis.telangana.gov.in/); [TMREIS About](https://tgmreistelangana.cgg.gov.in/aboutUs)

### Inferences
- KGBVs (about 5,300 functional), EMRS (about 480), JNVs (about 653), Sainik Schools and state welfare societies together make up a large share of UDISE+'s about 47,000 residential schools. The rest are mostly private boarding schools and state-run hostels or ashram schools (inference, not a sourced breakdown).

### Gaps
- No reliable count found for private boarding schools, or for the Indian Public Schools' Conference (IPSC) membership.
- No residential-society data for Andhra Pradesh or Odisha (for example, Odisha's ST/SC Development department residential schools).

## PM SHRI schools

### Takeaway
About 13,076 schools had been selected by August 2025 (9,373 of them rural), against a target of 14,500+. A 2026 report gives 13,092 across 36 States/UTs (as of July 2026). The outlay is Rs 27,360 crore over 5 years, with a central share of Rs 18,128 crore.

### Cited Findings
- 13,076 PM SHRI schools selected across 33 states, 9,373 of them rural (19 Aug 2025) — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/delhi/govt-highlights-rural-education-push-over-34k-cr-released-under-samagra-shiksha)
- As of July 2026, 13,092 schools selected across 36 States/UTs (about 90% of target); another report gives 12,079 across four phases (earlier) — [Organiser, 14 Sep 2026](https://organiser.org/2026/09/14/380419/bharat/how-pm-shri-schools-are-changing-the-way-bharat-thinks-about-education/) (search snippet; attribution of each figure not fully verified)
- Launched on 7 September 2022 with a target of more than 14,500 schools. Outlay Rs 27,360 crore over five years, central share Rs 18,128 crore. Components include smart classrooms, ICT labs, integrated science labs, Atal Tinkering Labs and skill labs — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/about/what-is-pm-shri-scheme); [Drishti IAS, Aug 2025](https://www.drishtiias.com/state-pcs-current-affairs/pm-schools-for-rising-india-pm-shri-scheme/print_manually)
- Uttar Pradesh has 1,888 PM SHRI schools selected, the most of any state — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/ground-reports/uttar-pradesh-pm-shri-scheme-schools-education-digital/2787733/)
- Separate from PM SHRI: under Samagra Shiksha, 1,76,669 ICT labs and 1,75,936 smart classrooms have been sanctioned to States/UTs (PRABANDH, Aug 2025) — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/delhi/govt-highlights-rural-education-push-over-34k-cr-released-under-samagra-shiksha)
- Digital facilities across all schools (UDISE+): computer facility 64.7% (2024-25) to 69.9% (2025-26); internet 63.5% to 67.4% — [Arun C. Mehta UDISE+ 2025-26 analysis](https://educationforallinindia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Has-Indias-School-enrolment-bottom-out-UDISEPlus-2025-26-aruncmehta.pdf)

### Inferences
- PM SHRI includes KVs and JNVs (620 JNVs planned) as well as state government schools. Tamil Nadu, Kerala and West Bengal were the long-standing holdouts on MoUs, which explains the gap to the target (holdouts are general knowledge, not re-verified).

### Gaps
- No official count of PM SHRI-specific ICT labs or smart classrooms completed.
- No 2026 PIB release with the national PM SHRI total.

## Board affiliation: CBSE, CISCE, IB, Cambridge, NIOS, state boards

### Takeaway
CBSE's SARAS directory shows 33,248 affiliated schools (accessed 6 November 2026), up from 29,179 in 2024 (Wikipedia). About 240 are abroad, in 28 countries. Approximate counts for other boards:
- **CISCE:** about 2,600-3,300 schools (sources disagree).
- **IB:** about 276-278 IB World Schools in India (2025), up from 192 in 2020.
- **Cambridge:** over 800 Cambridge schools in India (July 2025).

The rest of India's 14.67 lakh schools are mostly state-board affiliated.

### Cited Findings
- CBSE SARAS: "Total No. of Schools affiliated with CBSE: 33248" (undated live counter, accessed 6 Nov 2026). The page gives no category breakdown — [CBSE SARAS](https://saras.cbse.gov.in/saras/AffiliatedList/ListOfSchdirReport)
- Wikipedia infobox: 29,179 CBSE schools (2024). About 240 schools in 28 foreign countries, the most in the UAE, then Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and Oman. An older category breakdown lists 16,741 Independent, 3,011 Government, 1,138 KV, 595 JNV and 14 Central Tibetan schools (outdated, roughly 2018-19 era) — [Wikipedia: CBSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Board_of_Secondary_Education)
- Third-party snapshots: 28,577 affiliated (2025, 16 regions); over 30,786 (March 2025) — [Sarkari List](https://sarkarilist.in/cbse-affiliated-school-code-list/); [Bhavishya CBSE Consultant](https://bhavishyacbseconsultant.com/is-cbse-only-in-india/)
- CISCE: Wikipedia lists 3,300+ schools (2025). Pages citing the CISCE school locator say over 2,600 schools in India and abroad — [Wikipedia: CISCE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_for_the_Indian_School_Certificate_Examinations); [CISCE locator (unofficial domain)](https://cisceboard.org/Locate.html)
- IB: the IBO India country page lists 278 IB World Schools. The Federal (May 2025): from 192 in 2020 to 276 in 2025 — [IBO India](https://www.ibo.org/about-the-ib/the-ib-by-country-and-territory/i/india/); [The Federal](https://thefederal.com/category/news/ib-schools-india-growth-tier-2-global-education-186403)
- Cambridge (July 2025): South Asia passed 1,000 Cambridge schools, growing from 894 (2023-24) to 1,034 (2024-25). Over 75% are in India, which has "over 800" Cambridge schools; 53% of the region's schools are in Tier 1 cities — [Cambridge International news, 23 Jul 2025](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/news/news-details/view/cambridge-reaches-1000-schools-in-south-asia-20250723/)
- Context: UDISE+ 2025-26 total schools 14,66,682 (government 10,05,245; private unaided 3,41,689; aided 79,261) — [Arun C. Mehta UDISE+ 2025-26 analysis](https://educationforallinindia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Has-Indias-School-enrolment-bottom-out-UDISEPlus-2025-26-aruncmehta.pdf); [PIB UDISE+ 2024-25 release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2161543&reg=3&lang=2)

### Inferences
- CBSE affiliations grew by about 4,000 in roughly two years (29,179 in 2024 to 33,248 in late 2026), about 7% a year. This is consistent with continued private-school demand and new KVs and PM SHRI-linked schools.
- CBSE plus CISCE plus IB plus Cambridge together make up about 37,000-38,000 schools, roughly 2.5% of all schools. The remaining roughly 97% (about 14.3 lakh) are state-board or unaffiliated elementary schools (inference: elementary schools often have no board affiliation in UDISE+).

### Gaps
- No official current CBSE breakdown by type (KV, JNV, government, aided, independent, foreign); the only breakdown found is outdated.
- No NIOS accredited-institution count, and no UDISE+ board-wise school distribution, was found.
- No official CISCE count was fetched from cisce.org; the figures above come from secondary or unofficial pages.
