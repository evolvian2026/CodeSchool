# Verification: school counts, enrolment, teachers and state table

Scope: the Summary paragraph, the section "Government runs 69% of schools but teaches under half the pupils" (Tables 1-4 and text) and "Six private-heavy states hold half the private Classes 3-8 pool" (Table 5) of `docs/research/reports/India schools enrolment and infrastructure.md`. Checked 6 Nov 2026.

How it was checked:
- The EFA key-indicators PDF and the UDISE+ 2024-25 Table 2.1 PDF were downloaded and converted to text with `pdftotext`, then read row by row. Earlier notes relied on search summaries for these.
- Headline 2025-26 figures were matched against official PIB material: the release of 7 Jul 2026 (ID 2282141, as reproduced by EFA) and the PIB explainer of Aug 2026. No PIB or UDISE+ page gives management-wise counts, so those rest on the EFA compilation of the official tables.
- All derived arithmetic was recomputed: shares, sums, PTRs, the Classes 3-8 estimate and the state pools.
