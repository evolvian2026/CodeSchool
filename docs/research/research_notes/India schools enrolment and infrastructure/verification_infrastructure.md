# Verification: infrastructure and learning-support sections

Report checked: `docs/research/reports/India schools enrolment and infrastructure.md`, sections "Two in three schools are online, but one in five has a projector" (Tables 8-12) and "Print, TV and a parent's phone still carry learning" (Tables 13-14), plus the Budget statements. Checked on 6 November 2026. The report was not edited.

Method: each figure was checked against an official source where one could be reached (PIB, TRAI, Lok Sabha answers on sansad.in, the CIET/NCERT PDFs, the MoSPI press note). Then the URL the report cites was opened to confirm it says what is claimed. Some sites (business-standard.com, the BJET journal, pib.gov.in through WebFetch, eparlib.sansad.in) blocked automated access or refused the connection. Those figures were checked through other sources instead, as noted in each row.
