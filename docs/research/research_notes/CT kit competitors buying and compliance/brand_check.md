## 1. Pluralsight's "Code School": history and US trademark status

### Takeaway
Code School (Orlando, founded 2011 by Gregg Pollack) was bought by Pluralsight in January 2015 for $36M and was folded into Pluralsight around 2018. The two "CODE SCHOOL" word-mark applications found at the USPTO are both **abandoned** (dead), and one was filed with "CODE" and "SCHOOL" disclaimed. No live US registration of plain "CODE SCHOOL" was found. That reduces the risk of a dominant prior owner, but it also points to the term being weak or descriptive.

### Cited Findings
- Pluralsight acquired Code School on 26 Jan 2015 for $36 million. Code School was founded in 2011 by Gregg Pollack and based in Orlando, Florida. It had about 40,000 active learners, 1 million sign-ups and a team of 39, and at first was to stay a separate entity. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1110488); also [EdSurge](https://edsurge.com/n/2015-01-26-pluralsight-acquires-code-school-for-36-million), [Higher Ed Dive](https://www.highereddive.com/news/pluralsight-acquires-online-coding-school/357085/)

## 5. Descriptiveness: is "Code School" weak or unregistrable?

### Takeaway
Very likely descriptive (possibly generic) for coding education in both the US and India. The original Code School disclaimed both words, Silicon Valley Code School disclaimed "CODE SCHOOL", and an Indian "CODING SCHOOLING" Class 41 application was refused. Expect a 2(e)(1) refusal in the US and a Section 9(1)(b) objection in India for a plain word mark, unless distinctiveness has been acquired.

### Cited Findings
- US: a term that is merely descriptive is unregistrable on the Principal Register under Lanham Act §2(e)(1) absent acquired distinctiveness under §2(f). — [Revision Legal](https://revisionlegal.com/trademark/trademark-registration/merely-descriptive-office-actions-section-2e1/); [Alt Legal](https://www.altlegal.com/blog/a-distinct-category-how-to-overcome-a-2e1-refusal/)
- Code School, LLC's own CODE SCHOOL application disclaimed "CODE" and "SCHOOL". — [Justia 85929456](https://trademarks.justia.com/859/29/code-85929456.html)
- SILICON VALLEY CODE SCHOOL disclaims "CODE SCHOOL". — [Justia 88619982](https://trademarks.justia.com/886/19/silicon-valley-code-88619982.html)
- Analogous US example: the TTAB affirmed a §2(e)(1) refusal of CRASH COURSE for educational services (2002). — [USPTO OG Notice, 23 Jul 2002](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/com/sol/og/2002/week30/pattab9.htm)
- The mark CODE for educational services was refused under §2(d) as confusable with CODE2040, showing that even "code"-based education marks collide. — [LAIPLA](https://laipla.net/code-confusable-with-code2040-for-educational-services-says-ttab)
- India: "CODING SCHOOLING" (Class 41) was refused. — [RegisterKaro 5133442](https://www.registerkaro.in/trademark-search/coding-schooling-5133442)

### Inferences
- Even if "CodeSchool" registered as a composite or stylised mark, protection would be narrow. Competitors could use "code school" descriptively, and enforcement against similar names would be hard. This is the main brand risk: **weakness rather than infringement**.
- Writing it as one word ("CodeSchool") is unlikely to overcome descriptiveness. Telescoped descriptive terms are generally treated like their two-word form. This is a general principle and not verified against a specific case here.

### Gaps
- No USPTO office action or TTAB decision specifically on "CODE SCHOOL" was retrieved. The TSDR file of 85929456 would show whether a 2(e)(1) refusal was issued.
- No Indian Registry examination report on a "code school" mark was retrieved.

## 6. Recommendation inputs: risk level, alternatives, what an attorney should check

### Takeaway
**Risk level (preliminary): Moderate overall.** Infringement risk is low to moderate: no live exact registrations were found in the US or India, though the Indian register is unverified and Mycodeschool Labs is a near-identical name in the same field. Registrability and protectability risk is high, since the term is descriptive. "CodeSchool" alone is a weak brand that the founder could struggle to own, register or defend.

### Cited Findings
- No live US word-mark registration for CODE SCHOOL was found; both applications are abandoned. — [Justia 85929456](https://trademarks.justia.com/859/29/code-85929456.html); [Justia 86724562](https://trademarks.justia.com/867/24/code-86724562.html)
- Indian analogue refused: CODING SCHOOLING, Class 41. — [RegisterKaro](https://www.registerkaro.in/trademark-search/coding-schooling-5133442)
- Same-field Indian near-match: Mycodeschool Labs Pvt Ltd. — [Tofler](https://www.tofler.in/mycodeschool-labs-private-limited/company/U74900KA2013PTC072237/addinformation)
- A distinctive device or added element can help: "CODING CLUB" (device) is shown registered in Class 41 in India despite descriptive words. — [RegisterKaro 5125839](https://www.registerkaro.in/trademark-search/coding-club-5125839)

### Inferences (checklist for the trademark attorney; availability not asserted)
1. **IP India e-Register / public search**: wordmark and phonetic search for CODESCHOOL, CODE SCHOOL, KODE SCHOOL, KODSCHOOL, MYCODESCHOOL, CODE SKOOL, CODING SCHOOL and CODESCHOOL.AI in Classes 9, 16 (printed kits/workbooks), 28 (educational toys/kits, relevant to a CT kit), 41 and 42. Also check the well-known marks list.
2. **Mycodeschool Labs Pvt Ltd**: any TM filings, current use, and whether "mycodeschool" has a reputation that could support a passing-off claim in India.
3. **USPTO TSDR**: confirm 85929456 and 86724562 are dead and read their office actions. Search for any live Pluralsight-owned "CODE SCHOOL" logo marks. Review SILICON VALLEY CODE SCHOOL (88619982) status and THE CODER SCHOOL family.
4. **WIPO Global Brand Database / Madrid**: international registrations containing CODE SCHOOL, plus EUIPO, UKIPO and IP Australia if expansion is planned.
5. **MCA21 name check** (and Zauba / The Company Check) for companies and LLPs containing CODESCHOOL / CODE SCHOOL, plus a common-law sweep (Google Maps, JustDial, Instagram, YouTube) for Indian academies trading as "Code School".
6. **Domains**: WHOIS/RDAP for codeschool.com, .in, .co.in, .ai and .org. **App stores**: Google Play and App Store title and developer searches.
7. **Strategy**: file a distinctive composite (a coined element plus logo) in Classes 41, 9, 42 and 28. Use "code school" only descriptively, as a tagline or sub-brand.
- Ways to reduce risk (to be searched, not asserted as available): add a coined or arbitrary prefix or suffix (e.g., an invented word, a mascot name, a Sanskrit/Hindi coinage) so that "code school" becomes a descriptor. Use a distinctive logo or device mark. Or pick a fully coined name. Search each candidate across the same classes, the MCA register, domains and app stores.

### Gaps
- The risk rating is provisional until the IP India and MCA searches above are run directly.
