# Associations, clusters and events that reach many Indian private / CBSE school leaders at once

Scope: networks and events through which a vendor of a CBSE CT&AI programme for Classes 3-8 (launch April 2027) can reach principals and managements in bulk. Researched 10 Nov 2026. Only organisation-level contacts are given (office emails, phone numbers, designations). Personal names and personal contact details were left out on purpose. Anything not confirmed by a primary source is marked UNVERIFIED.

## 1. CBSE Sahodaya School Complexes (SSCs)

### Takeaway
Sahodayas are CBSE-recognised voluntary clusters of neighbouring CBSE schools. Each is run by elected principals who serve as President, Secretary and Treasurer. CBSE counts "more than 250" complexes. A complex must register on CBSE's Sahodaya micro-site (sahodayaschools.org) to be recognised. The National Annual Conference is CBSE's single largest principal gathering, with 1,000+ school leaders at the 2025 Dubai edition. No 2026 edition (the 32nd) could be found as of Nov 2026, and CBSE publishes no public list of Sahodaya contacts. The workable routes are: (a) approach individual Sahodayas through their own websites or office bearers' schools, and (b) partner with or present at the annual conference, which the host Sahodaya organises.

### Cited Findings
- Sahodayas were created in 1986. CBSE defines a Sahodaya as "a group of neighbourhood schools voluntarily coming together to share their innovative practices in all aspects of school education including curriculum design, evaluation and pedagogy" and to provide support services to teachers and students. — [CBSE Circular Acad-40/2022, 17 Mar 2022](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2022/40_Circular_2022.pdf)
- Scale: "At present there are more than 250 Sahodaya School Complexes across the country" (2022). An older CBSE report from the 2019 conference said "around 200 active" SSCs. — [Circular Acad-40/2022](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2022/40_Circular_2022.pdf); [CBSE Sahodaya Report (25th conference)](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/report/Sahodaya_%20Report.pdf)
- How a Sahodaya is organised:
  - 5 to 10 geographically close schools form a complex. The principals elect a President, Secretary, Treasurer and other office bearers for 2-3 year rotating terms.
  - A district can have more than one SSC.
  - Each SSC has bye-laws, a joint bank account audited every year, an optional website and an annual action plan, and members are expected to meet at least monthly.
  - Source: [Circular Acad-40/2022](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2022/40_Circular_2022.pdf)
- Registration: every SSC, new or existing, had to register on the CBSE Sahodaya micro-website (www.sahodayaschools.org/Sahodaya_New/registration.php) by 30 Apr 2022. "Sahodaya School Complexes not registered on the CBSE Sahodaya micro website will not be recognized by the Board." Office-bearer changes must be updated there. — [Circular Acad-40/2022](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2022/40_Circular_2022.pdf)
- Hosting the national conference: SSCs apply to host at sahodayaschools.org/Sahodaya_New/ehl.php. If several apply for the same year, CBSE picks the host at random. Official query email: sahodaya@cbseshiksha.in. CBSE Academics office: directoracad.cbse@nic.in. — [Circular Acad-40/2022](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2022/40_Circular_2022.pdf)
- Sahodaya size varies by city. A news report says the Indore Sahodaya has over 100 member schools (secondary source, not checked by me). — [Free Press Journal (via search summary)](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/indore/indore-schools-welcomes-new-changes-in-sahodaya-movement-of-cbse)
- Regional organisation: Sahodayas are named by city or district (for example the Delhi-NCR group: Indraprastha Sahodaya, Sankalp Sahodaya, South Delhi Sahodaya, Sarv Sahodaya, Delhi Sahodaya Schools Complex). Overseas there is the Emirates Sahodaya and the Gulf Sahodaya chapters. — [CBSE Sahodaya Report](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/report/Sahodaya_%20Report.pdf); [CBSE Notification 65/2025](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Notifications/2025/65_Notification_2025.pdf)
- 2025 conference (31st Annual and 1st International Conference of Sahodaya School Complexes):
  - Dates and venue: 4-5 Nov 2025, Dubai, UAE (moved from 3-4 Nov). Hosted by the Emirates Sahodaya Schools Complex UAE with the Gulf Sahodaya, under CBSE. Venue reported as Grand Hyatt Dubai.
  - CBSE notification: registration portal opened 28 July 2025 (cbseinternationalsahodayaconference.com). The notification was sent to all Regional Directors and COE heads to circulate to schools.
  - Sources: [CBSE Notification 65/2025](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Notifications/2025/65_Notification_2025.pdf); [The Peninsula Qatar](https://s.thepeninsula.qa/article/17/09/2025/conference-of-sahodaya-school-complexes-to-be-held-on-nov-4-5); [DD News](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/first-international-sahodaya-conference-opens-in-dubai-uniting-global-indian-educators/)
- 2025 theme and turnout: the theme was "Rooted in Wisdom, Rising with Vision: Reimagining Education through NEP 2020". Coverage reports 1,000+ school leaders. CBSE used the event to announce a Global Curriculum for its overseas schools from April 2026. — [News On AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/cbse-hosts-first-international-sahodaya-conference-in-dubai/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/uae/education/cbse-schools-in-uae-gcc-to-follow-new-global-curriculum-from-april-2026-1.500334377)
- Format precedent from the 2019 (25th) conference:
  - Delhi, 1-2 Nov 2019. Co-hosted by the Delhi-NCR Sahodayas and NPSC.
  - Registration fee Rs 7,500 per participant, and fees collected by the host Sahodaya are "subject to third party audit as laid down by the Board".
  - The programme had best-practice case-study exhibits by schools and a "Book of Essays" by teachers and principals.
  - Source: [CBSE Sahodaya Report](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/report/Sahodaya_%20Report.pdf)
- The conference website for the 2025 edition now returns HTTP 404, so sponsor packages could not be retrieved. — fetch of cbseinternationalsahodayaconference.com/home, 10 Nov 2026

### Inferences
- Since CBSE has made CT&AI the 2026-27 teacher-training theme (Section 4), Sahodaya-level capacity-building workshops this year are likely to be about CT&AI. A vendor offering free, CBSE-aligned teacher workshops through Sahodayas is the most scalable way in: 250+ clusters of 5-100+ schools each.
- Vendor engagement at the national conference most likely works through the host Sahodaya (sponsorship and stalls), not CBSE directly. Fees are audited and the event is CBSE-branded, so expect the host to limit overtly commercial content. Pitch as a "knowledge partner" or workshop provider, not just an exhibitor. (Inference from the format, not a documented sponsor policy.)
- City Sahodayas usually run their own annual principals' meets, inter-school events and teacher-training days. These are reached by contacting the Sahodaya President's or Secretary's school office.

### Gaps
- No date, venue or host could be found for a 2026 (32nd) National Sahodaya Conference. Searches for "32nd" and "2026" Sahodaya conference returned nothing as of 10 Nov 2026. Check cbseacademic.nic.in notifications. UNVERIFIED whether one is planned.
- CBSE does not publish a downloadable list of Sahodayas with office-bearer contacts. sahodayaschools.org is a registration portal, and I did not confirm it has a public directory.
- No published sponsorship or exhibitor rate card for the Sahodaya conference was found. The 2019 and 2025 documents mention only delegate fees.
- No current official count of complexes after 2022 was found. The figure may now be well above 250.

## 2. Private school associations (national, Delhi and state)

### Takeaway
There are two national umbrellas. NISA claims about 1 lakh budget private schools through 65 state associations. NPSC (about 195-210 CBSE schools) and IPSC (about 80 elite residential schools) are elite networks. Below them, the useful bulk channels are state management associations, which are mostly advocacy bodies focused on fees and RTE disputes. Examples: MESTA in Maharashtra (about 18,000-20,000 trustee members claimed), KAMS in Karnataka (600-1,900 schools), TRSMA in Telangana and APPUSMA in Andhra Pradesh. Most run annual conventions and have no formal vendor programme. COBSE is a council of school boards, not of schools, and is not a principal channel.

Older issues of its newsletter cited about 36,500 schools in 21 states, so the figures have grown or are inconsistent. — [nisaindia.org](https://nisaindia.org/); [NISA Namaskar vol 1 issue 5](https://nisaindia.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/nisanamaskar-volume-1-issue-5.pdf)

  - Advocacy, a "Quality Program" (partners named: LaunchMyCareer, eNREAD, Educational Initiatives, Finnish Education Exploration), research, and membership for state associations and "Associated Members".
  - Media: EDTalks and the bi-monthly magazine *NISA Namaskar* (Rs 590 per year).
  - Partner logos on the site include Worldreader, EduFinance, Lead and Edumarket.
  - No events were listed at the time of fetch.
  - Source: [nisaindia.org](https://nisaindia.org/)
- Office contact: nisa@nisaindia.org; +91 7669078760; Unit 52-53, Vidya Nagar Colony, Nanhera Road, Ambala Cantt, Haryana 133004. — [nisaindia.org](https://nisaindia.org/)

- State affiliates seen in sources: Telangana (TRSMA links), APPUSMA (Andhra Pradesh; NISA attended its conference), and the Federation of Private Schools and Welfare Association, Haryana (in NISA meetings, 2021). **COBSE – Council of Boards of School Education in India**
- A voluntary association of school boards, about 57 member boards per a low-reliability Scribd document. It acts as a service agency and information bureau for boards. Website: cobse.in. Its members are boards, not schools. — [Teachmint glossary](https://www.teachmint.com/glossary/?p=5232); [DEV profile](https://dev.to/cobseindia/cobse-the-council-of-boards-of-school-education-in-india-1gip)

### Inferences
- For a CBSE CT&AI product, the best-fit associations are NPSC (CBSE-only, and a former co-host of the Sahodaya conference) and city Sahodayas. NISA and the state budget-school associations reach large numbers of schools, but many of those schools are state-board and price-sensitive. They suit a low-cost CBSE-aligned tier, or NISA's "Quality Program" partner route, which already includes edtech partners such as Educational Initiatives.
- These state associations are mainly lobbying bodies. In 2026 they are focused on fee regulation, RTE dues and inspections, so a pitch tying CT&AI to CBSE compliance and the 2026-27 mandate is more likely to work than a pure sales pitch. Sponsoring their annual conventions (MESTA, APPUSMA, TRSMA) is the usual way in, but no rate cards were found.
- IPSC's January conclave is small (about 80 heads) and premium. It is better for referrals and credibility than for volume.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found of a "Federation of Public Schools" (Delhi), a "Principals' Progressive Schools Association" or a "National Federation of Private Schools Associations". These may not exist under those names. UNVERIFIED.
- No association-level details were found for Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan or Punjab. Hindi-language searches or NISA's state-association page would be needed.
- No sponsorship or exhibitor terms were found for any association convention.
- Official websites, emails and phones were not retrieved for KAMS, MESTA, TRSMA, APPUSMA, the TN associations or the Action Committee. The Action Committee website is known: actcommittee.com.

## 3. Major education events and conferences where principals gather (2026-2027)

### Takeaway
DIDAC India (25-27 Nov 2026, BIEC Bengaluru) is the only large trade show with a published exhibitor price list: raw space USD 520 per sqm, shell scheme USD 590 per sqm (minimum 9 sqm), plus 18% GST, with sponsorships from USD 500 to 14,700. Its 2025 edition had 27,546 visitors, 417 exhibitors and 2,500+ senior leaders in its conference sessions.

Other gatherings are smaller, invitation-only or award-led:
- Elets World Education Summit: invite-only, about 100 founders and owners per K-12 forum.
- EducationWorld India School Rankings awards conclave: Delhi, mid-October, billed as the largest gathering of K-12 leaders.
- IPSC conclave: January.
- GETI Ed Leadership Roundtable: Lucknow, June.
- Assorted regional "summits", some run by edtech vendors.

## 4. CBSE's own channels: Regional Offices, Centres of Excellence, CT&AI webinars and training cascade

### Takeaway
CBSE sends circulars through its Regional Offices (ROs) and trains teachers through Centres of Excellence (COEs), one attached to each RO; about 22 COEs are listed. The cbse.gov.in RO page lists 16 regions, but the COE page also names newer ROs (Ahmedabad, Gurugram, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Lucknow, Raipur, Ranchi, Vijayawada).

CT&AI is now mandated for Classes 3-8 from 2026-27 (Circular Acad-18/2026, 9 Apr 2026). The design is platform-independent resource books used alongside NCERT Mathematics. CBSE ran a national webinar for it, and "Computational Thinking and Understanding AI" is the 2026-27 teacher-training theme. COE capacity-building programmes are the official cascade a vendor would want to support, but CBSE stresses "without dependence on any platform".

### Cited Findings
**CT&AI mandate and training**
- Circular Acad-18/2026 (9 Apr 2026):
  - CBSE "has released the curriculum framework for CT & AI, for students of classes 3rd to 8th, with the aim of creating AI-literate learners by 2030. The curriculum mandates CT + AI + Ethics + Unplugged Learning." CBSE integrates CT with AI "without dependence on any platform".
  - Student and Teacher Handbooks for each class are "not designed as textbooks, but rather as a companion to the NCERT Mathematics textbooks", following the same chapter sequence.
  - Older AI courses are discontinued for Class IX from 2026-27, and NCERT will give CT&AI modules for Classes 9-12.
  - Source: [CBSE Circular Acad-18/2026](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2026/18_Circular_2026.pdf)
- The CBSE CT&AI page carries the curriculum PDF, the circular, a recorded CBSE webinar on CT&AI for Classes III-VIII (2026-27) on YouTube Live with downloadable slides, and 12 handbooks (student and teacher, Classes 3-8). — [cbseacademic.nic.in/ct-ai.html](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/ct-ai.html)
- Teacher-training theme for 2026-27: "Computational Thinking and Understanding Artificial Intelligence". Schools are asked to integrate CT&AI into maths and science and to run workshops for teachers and students. This comes from secondary reports; the exact circular number was not verified. — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/education/cbse-to-implement-computational-thinking-ai-curriculum-for-classes-38-from-202627-announces-teacher-training-theme-with-focus-on-coding-future-skills); [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/cbse-asks-schools-integrate-computational-thinking-ai-curriculum-maths-science-subjects-conduct-workshops-teachers-students-board/amp)
- Hours (50 per year for Classes 3-5, 100 for Classes 6-8) are reported only by secondary sites and are UNVERIFIED. — [aglasem](https://news.aglasem.com/cbse-ct-ai-handbook-class-3-to-8/)
- CBSE Academics office (HQ): CBSE Integrated Office Complex, Sector-23, Phase-1, Dwarka, New Delhi 110077; tel 011-24050612; directoracad.cbse@nic.in. The 2022 address was Shiksha Sadan, Rouse Avenue (tel 011-23212603). — [Circular Acad-18/2026](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2026/18_Circular_2026.pdf); [Circular Acad-40/2022](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2022/40_Circular_2022.pdf)
- Distribution: CBSE academic circulars and notifications go to "All Regional Directors/Regional Officers of CBSE with the request to send this circular to all the Heads of the affiliated schools" and to "All Head(s)/In-Charge(s), Centre of Excellence". — [Notification 65/2025](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Notifications/2025/65_Notification_2025.pdf); [Circular 18/2026](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2026/18_Circular_2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- CBSE explicitly calls CT&AI platform-independent and ties it to the NCERT maths books. A commercial programme is therefore safest positioned as a supplement that maps chapter by chapter to the CBSE CT&AI handbooks and the 2026-27 training theme, not as a replacement.
- Each COE runs region-wide capacity-building programmes. Offering certified CT&AI trainer resources or co-hosted workshops through COEs, or through Sahodayas that request COE resource persons, is the official multiplier. Any formal tie-up would need CBSE Academics (directoracad.cbse@nic.in) or Training Unit approval. No public policy for vendor partnerships with COEs was found.
- The RO and COE contacts above are official generic office routes. Several are hosted on Gmail but appear on cbse.gov.in itself.

### Gaps
- The exact current number of CBSE Regional Offices could not be confirmed. The RO page shows 16 regions, but the COE page names at least 8 newer ROs, which suggests about 24. The RO page's jurisdiction notes are stale.
- The 2026-27 training-theme circular (number and date) and the hours per class were not checked against the primary document.
- No details were found on CBSE's master-trainer cascade for CT&AI (number of master trainers, how COEs schedule CT&AI sessions). These may sit in the CBSE Training portal (cbseit.in / training calendar), which was not retrieved.
- Address and phone for RO Chandigarh and RO Patna, and contacts for the Kolkata, Vijayawada and Gurugram ROs, were not captured.
