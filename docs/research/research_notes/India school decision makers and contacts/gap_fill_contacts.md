# Gap-fill round: organisation-level contact routes for Indian school networks, the NVS Hyderabad and Patna regional offices, and state education offices (as of Nov 2026)

**Scope.** These notes add to `private_chains_decision_makers.md` and `government_central_contacts.md` and do not repeat what those files already hold. The earlier files already cover DPS Society, Podar, Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, DAV CMC, Chinmaya, Jaipuria, KVS, NVS (6 of 8 regional offices), AWES, AEES, NESTS and Sainik Schools. Where a network was only partly covered before (Ryan, VIBGYOR, Orchids, Lighthouse, Zee Learn, Edify, Narayana, Sri Chaitanya, Vidya Bharati, GD Goenka, Amrita), this file adds only the new contact data.

**Contacts collected.** Organisation-level only: offices, role mailboxes and switchboards. No individuals' details were collected.

**How the sources were read.** Most facts come from web-search result summaries that pointed to the cited pages. Only some of those pages were fetched directly. Anything not confirmed on an official page is marked UNVERIFIED.

**Budget.** The round used about 25 tool calls, so several networks have no data. They are listed in the Gaps sections.

## Q3. State education contact routes for the likely launch states, and private-school fee regulators

### Takeaway
Official portals were confirmed for only some offices:
- SCERTs in UP, Delhi, Haryana and MP.
- The Samagra Shiksha State Project Office in Gujarat (address, phones and role emails).
- UP's Basic Education portal.

For the other states, the likely official domains are listed below as UNVERIFIED leads, not confirmed this session.

Every launch state with known fee legislation regulates fees through committees at the school or district level: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Punjab, Haryana, Delhi, Tamil Nadu, and UP (bill stage). None of these committees is a vendor-approval authority. Their relevance is that charges for books and kits fall under fee and "no compulsory purchase" scrutiny.

**Fee regulation laws**
- **Delhi:** the Delhi School Education (Transparency in Fixation and Regulation of Fees) Act, 2025 covers 1,677 private and aided schools.
  - On 2 July 2026 the government directed all private unaided schools to set up School Level Fee Regulation Committees by 15 July.
  - The Act is in litigation. The Supreme Court backed the aim of fee regulation but questioned the "hurried implementation", and the High Court had earlier extended the committee deadline. — [Tribune: new fee framework](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/delhi/govt-rolls-out-new-fee-regulation-framework-for-private-schools/); [NewKerala: FRCs by July 15](https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/delhi-govt-directs-private-unaided-schools-set-up-113.htm); [Careers360: Supreme Court on Delhi fee rules](https://news.careers360.com/supreme-court-backs-delhi-private-school-fee-regulation-questions-govts-hurried-implementation-fees-high-academic-year/amp)
- **Tamil Nadu:** the Tamil Nadu Schools (Regulation of Collection of Fee) Amendment Act, 2026 was passed on 26 January 2026. It sets up a 7-member fee committee chaired by a retired High Court judge, and takes effect when notified. — [EducationPost: TN amendment](https://educationpost.in/news/education/tamil-nadu-assembly-passes-amendment-to-regulate-private-school-fee)
- **Gujarat:** the Gujarat Self-Financed Schools (Regulation of Fees) Act, 2017 sets fee ceilings by level. Schools that want to charge more apply to a Fee Regulatory Committee headed by a retired district judge. — [Tribune archive: Gujarat passes bill](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/nation/gujarat-passes-bill-to-regulate-pvt-schools-fee-384858); [CCS: Fee laws](https://old.ccs.in/fee-laws-hunt-model-state)
- **Maharashtra:** the Maharashtra Educational Institutions (Regulation of Fee) Act, 2011 requires each unaided school to have a PTA-based executive committee to approve fees. — [Careers360: states and fee hikes](https://news.careers360.com/state-governments-and-battle-against-fee-hikes-in-private-schools)
- **Rajasthan:** the Rajasthan Schools (Regulation of Fee) Act, 2016 sets up a School Level Fee Committee, with escalation to a Divisional Fee Regulatory Committee. — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/state-governments-and-battle-against-fee-hikes-in-private-schools)
- **Punjab:** the Punjab Regulation of Fee of Unaided Educational Institutions Act, 2016 caps annual increases at about 8%. — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/state-governments-and-battle-against-fee-hikes-in-private-schools)
- **Haryana:** the Haryana School Education (Amendment) Rules, 2014 set up a divisional Fee and Fund Regulatory Committee chaired by the Divisional Commissioner. — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/state-governments-and-battle-against-fee-hikes-in-private-schools)
- **Uttar Pradesh:** the UP Self-financed Independent Schools (Regulation of Fees) Bill, 2017 covers schools charging over Rs 20,000 a year, through zonal committees under Divisional Commissioners. Whether it was enacted was not confirmed in this round. — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/state-governments-and-battle-against-fee-hikes-in-private-schools)
- **Karnataka:** a 2017 amendment proposal would fine schools Rs 10 lakh for charging above the prescribed fee. It was a proposal at the time. — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/state-governments-and-battle-against-fee-hikes-in-private-schools)
- **Model framework:** NCPCR published a model fee-regulation framework for unaided private schools. — [NCPCR model framework (PDF)](http://opaf.in/img/documents/FEE_Regulation_Framework_NCPCR.pdf)

- **What this means for a vendor:**
  - Fee committees do not approve vendors. But where a programme fee or kit is charged to parents, it becomes part of the fee structure that SLFCs and FRCs review.
  - Several states and the Delhi DoE also restrict schools from forcing purchases of books or kits from a named vendor.
  - Pricing should therefore either sit inside tuition, so the school buys, or be offered as optional open-market purchase. Do not make it a compulsory separate charge.
- **For the state route:** start with the SPD (Samagra Shiksha) office's ICT or innovation component and the SCERT's curriculum and teacher-training wing. Gujarat's SPD office is the only one with confirmed official phones and emails from this round.
