# Printed CBSE CT&AI books, Classes 3-8 (2026-27): a title-by-title teardown

Research date: 10 November 2026. Scope: what the printed CT&AI series actually contain, beyond the prices already in `reports/CT kit competitors buying and compliance.md`.

**Overall caveat.** I did not open a single look-inside preview or sample page of a commercial CT&AI book. Publisher and bookseller product pages for these titles carry almost no contents information. Most of what follows is therefore either (a) **[LISTING]**, metadata from a product page; (b) **[MARKETING]**, publisher claims; or (c) **[CBSE]**, the free CBSE handbook that the commercial books shadow. No chapter list for any commercial CT&AI title was found. Several fetches failed (Arihant and Kitabkopy returned HTTP 429, Amazon.in returned 503, and the OUP site search returned 404), so those gaps are access failures, not proof that nothing exists.

## 1. Reference point: what the CBSE handbook structure looks like (the benchmark each commercial book is measured against)

### Takeaway
CBSE's free Class 6 Student Handbook is split in two. Part 1 is computational thinking (CT), keyed chapter by chapter to the NCERT Class 6 maths textbook. Part 2 has four AI chapters, each paired with an "ethical awareness" theme. Hours are set at 20 for AI concepts and 40 for two 20-hour interdisciplinary projects, assessed through presentations, assignments and reflective journals. Any commercial book claiming "aligned" alignment should reproduce this skeleton.

### Cited Findings
- **[CBSE]** The Class 6 Student Handbook (First Edition, March 2026) has a two-part table of contents. Part 1, Computational Thinking, has 12 entries: Introduction, How to Use this Book, then Patterns in Mathematics, Lines and Angles, Number Play, Data Handling and Presentation, Prime Time, Perimeter and Area, Fractions, Playing with Constructions, Symmetry, and The Other Side of Zero. These are the NCERT Class 6 maths chapters, in order. — [CBSE Class 6 Student Handbook PDF](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/CTAI_Pri6SH_2026-27.pdf)
- **[CBSE]** Part 2, Artificial Intelligence, has four chapters, each with an "ethical awareness" column:
  1. Introduction to AI and Everyday Examples: meaning of AI, AI vs automation, human vs machine intelligence, types of learning. Ethics theme: responsible usage.
  2. Basic Data Concepts. Ethics theme: data handling care.
  3. Simple Pattern Recognition and Decision Making. Ethics theme: logical thinking.
  4. Ethics and Digital Responsibility: online safety, privacy, password safety, digital footprints. Ethics theme: online behaviour.
  — [CBSE Class 6 Student Handbook](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/CTAI_Pri6SH_2026-27.pdf)
- **[CBSE]** Class 6 hours: "Introductory AI Concepts: 20 hours" and "Interdisciplinary Projects: 40 hours (20 hours each for two projects)". Assessment uses "Performance-Based Tools: Project presentations, assignments, and reflective journals". Project-based learning has students use "AI tools and data analysis to create solutions for community or fictional city issues". — [CBSE Class 6 Student Handbook](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/CTAI_Pri6SH_2026-27.pdf)
- **[CBSE]** The CT part is designed to be "used alongside regular classroom teaching. Since it follows the same chapter sequence, the Mathematics teacher can seamlessly integrate it into daily instruction." — [CBSE Class 6 Student Handbook](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/CTAI_Pri6SH_2026-27.pdf)
- A Scribd upload titled "Download File" is the CBSE Class 6 Student Handbook (84 pages), not a commercial book. Its chapter list (Patterns in Mathematics, Lines and Angles, and so on) is CBSE's own. — [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/1069671951/Download-File)
- The earlier report found that the CBSE handbooks contain no coding, no project materials and no rubrics, and that only Class 8 has hands-on AI. — [CBSE Class 8 Student Handbook](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/CTAI_Pri8SH_2026-27.pdf) (as summarised in `reports/CT kit competitors buying and compliance.md`)

### Inferences
- A commercial book that is "aligned to the CBSE CT&AI curriculum" will most likely copy this skeleton: CT chapters named after the NCERT maths chapters, plus four AI chapters with an ethics strand in Classes 6-8. Whether any of them adds coding, project briefs or rubrics on top is exactly what this research could not verify from samples.

### Gaps
- I did not extract the Class 3-5 and Class 7-8 handbook tables of contents this round. The Class 7 and Class 8 PDFs are at the same CBSE path (`CTAI_Pri7SH_2026-27.pdf`, `CTAI_Pri8SH_2026-27.pdf`).

## 4. Orange Education: "Artificial Intelligence" (Touchpad) and the 2026-27 catalogue
