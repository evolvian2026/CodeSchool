# Development and Operating Costs for a CodeSchool-type Programme in India (2025-2026)

Research date: 10 November 2026. All rupee figures are in INR. Where a source quotes USD, a conversion at an assumed ~INR 88 per USD is shown and labelled as an estimate (exchange rate not sourced; check at time of use). Most price sources found are vendor blogs, marketplace listings or crowd-sourced salary sites. Treat them as indicative ranges, not quotes. Primary or official sources are flagged where available.

## Content development: rates for writers/SMEs, instructional designers, illustrators, DTP, editors, Hindi translation; cost per workbook title and 6-book series

### Takeaway
No published Indian K-12 publishing rate card was found. Proxy benchmarks: chapter writing about INR 15,000-40,000 per 5,000-7,000-word chapter; DTP about INR 35-50 per page at the budget end (marketplace listings go up to INR 100-500 per page); Hindi translation about INR 1-8 per word. Per-illustration rupee rates could not be verified. Inferred cost of one illustrated 96-128-page K-8 workbook, all-in: about INR 3-10 lakh. Inferred cost of a 6-level workbook series plus teacher script books: about INR 25-75 lakh (estimate).

### Cited Findings
**Writers / subject-matter experts**
- A promotional Indian education-career blog lists a textbook chapter of 5,000-7,000 words at INR 15,000-40,000 per chapter (roughly INR 2-6 per word). Blog posts are listed at INR 3,000-10,000. Not a publisher survey. — [Frontlines Media](https://frontlinesmedia.in/?p=42438)
- Freelance listing: a writer quotes INR 150 per question for school/college Q&A preparation, and about INR 250 (USD 5) per hour. — [Freelancer.in project listing](https://www.freelancer.in/projects/educational-research/indian-social-studies-content)
- EdTech SEO article rates of INR 800-1,200 per 1,500-2,500-word article show the low end of the market. This is not comparable to curriculum writing. — [Freelancer.com.au listing](https://www.freelancer.com.au/projects/seo-writing/seo-content-writer-for-edtech)
- Delhi Press is recruiting freelance textbook curriculum writers for Grades 1-2 (English, Maths, EVS, Hindi). The listing gives no rate. This confirms the freelance-writer model is standard in Indian K-12 publishing. — [Delhi Press careers](https://www.delhipress.in/careers/freelance-textbook-curriculum-writers-grades-1-2)

**DTP / typesetting**
- An Indian self-publishing service's worked example uses INR 35 per page for standard text and INR 50 per page for books with illustrations, tables and flowcharts. — [Cinnamon Teal blog](https://blog.cinnamonteal.in/?p=1481)
- IndiaMART vendors list typesetting/DTP at roughly INR 100-500 per page. Some multilingual DTP listings are around INR 200 per page. — [IndiaMART typesetting](https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/typesetting-service.html); [IndiaMART multilingual DTP](https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/multilingual-desktop-publishing-services.html)
- A typesetting firm quotes "starting at INR 20 per page" and notes that multilingual pricing varies. — [MAP Systems](https://mapsystemsindia.com/pre-press-services/typesetting-services.html)
- National Translation Mission (government-linked; document date unclear) rate sheet: INR 12 per page for English with IPA, INR 15 for bilingual text, INR 20 for trilingual text, and INR 25 per complex table/figure. The same sheet covers rates for translators, copy editors, proofreaders and reviewers. — [NTM rate sheet PDF](https://www.ntm.org.in/announcements/Rates%20for%20translators,%20data%20entry,%20copy%20eidtor,%20proof%20reader%20and%20reviewers.pdf)

**Hindi translation**
- 2025 experience-based ranges: INR 0.50-2 per word (beginner), INR 2-5 (intermediate), INR 5-10 (experienced), INR 10-20+ (specialist). English-Hindi experts are listed at INR 3-8 per word. — [AIM India](https://aimindia.in/?p=14991)
- Industry guide: technical INR 1.50-4 per word, legal/financial INR 3-6, marketing/transcreation INR 2-5. — [VerboLabs](https://www.verbolabs.com/?p=21738)
- 2025 guide: professional translation INR 1-2.50 per word, and machine translation from INR 0.25 per word (language pair not specified). — [Reverie](https://reverieinc.com/?p=1287629)
- An individual ProZ translator profile lists INR 3 per word or INR 150 per hour. — [ProZ profile](https://www.proz.com/profile/4693492)
- USD-priced agencies: Hindi human translation about USD 0.07-0.11 per word, and machine translation plus post-editing about USD 0.03-0.05 per word. — [The Word Point](https://thewordpoint.com/languages/hindi-translation-services)

**Illustration**
- No reliable per-illustration rupee rate was found. Indian illustrators on Freelancer.com list about USD 20-30 per hour. Fiverr Indian children's-book illustrators list USD 15-30 per hour. — [Freelancer.com hire illustrator](https://www.freelancer.com/hire/illustrator); [Fiverr profile example](https://www.fiverr.com/arunodoyart)
- One Fiverr children's-book gig is priced at USD 100-200 (unclear whether this is per book or per image). — [Fiverr gig](https://fiverr.com/spykehead/do-illustrations-for-childrens-book)
- Solohourly's India pages give conflicting figures: a minimum of about INR 96 per hour, and book illustration at USD 2,000-10,000 per project. These are unreliable. — [Solohourly India illustrator](https://solohourly.com/rates/illustrator-rates-in-india)

**Editors / proofreaders / instructional designers**
- No India-specific per-page editing or instructional-design freelance rate was found in this search. The NTM rate sheet above covers copy editors and proofreaders for government translation work. — [NTM rate sheet PDF](https://www.ntm.org.in/announcements/Rates%20for%20translators,%20data%20entry,%20copy%20eidtor,%20proof%20reader%20and%20reviewers.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Estimate: one student workbook title.** Assumptions: 96-128 pages, 10-12 chapters, 15,000-25,000 words, 60-150 illustrations.
  - Writing/SME: 10-12 chapters x INR 15k-40k = INR 1.5-4.8 lakh.
  - Instructional design/review and editing/proofreading: an assumed 20-30% uplift on writing cost = INR 0.3-1.4 lakh.
  - Illustration: about INR 1,300-8,000 per spot illustration, inferred from USD 15-30 per hour at 1-3 hours per image. For 60-150 images this is INR 0.8-12 lakh. This is the most uncertain line, and full-page or character work costs more.
  - DTP: 128 pages x INR 50-300 = INR 6,400-38,000.
  - Hindi translation: 20,000 words x INR 2-6 = INR 40,000-1.2 lakh, plus Hindi review at about 30-50% of translation cost.
  - Overall: roughly INR 3-10 lakh per title if illustration is kept moderate.
- **Estimate: teacher script book per level.** It is usually text-heavy with few illustrations, so roughly INR 1-3 lakh each.
- **Estimate: 6-level series.** Six workbooks plus six teacher books comes to about INR 25-75 lakh one-time. Costs go down if a shared illustration and character library is reused across levels.
- **Assumption:** content refresh is about 10-20% of the original cost per year.
- AI-assisted drafting and machine translation with post-editing (about USD 0.03-0.05 per word, per The Word Point) can lower writing and translation costs, but human SME and Hindi review remain necessary.

### Gaps
- No Indian K-12 publisher rate card was found for per-page or per-chapter writing, instructional design, editing or proofreading. The Frontlines figure is a promotional blog.
- No reliable rupee per-illustration rate was found. Getting 3-5 quotes from Indian illustrators or studios is recommended.
- No Translators Association of India rate card was found in this search.
- Exchange rate used for conversions (about INR 88/USD) is an assumption.

## Teacher training delivery: cost per in-person orientation, online session, and per teacher trained

### Takeaway
Public benchmarks are low. The Samagra Shiksha norm was INR 315 per teacher per day for Classes I-VIII in-service training (Delhi, 2018-19; may since have been revised). CBSE capacity-building fees were historically INR 600-1,000 per teacher per day, and many 2025-2026 CBSE programmes are free. For a private provider the main cost is trainer salary plus travel. Inferred direct cost is about INR 300-1,500 per teacher for a 1-day in-person orientation, and well under INR 200 per teacher for online sessions.

### Cited Findings
- Samagra Shiksha (Delhi progress report, 2018-19 proposal): in-service teacher training for Classes I-VIII at INR 315 per teacher per day; head-teacher training (Classes IX-XII) at INR 1,600 per HM per day; educational administrator training at INR 1,000 per trainee per day. — [Delhi Samagra Shiksha – Training for In-Service Teachers PDF](https://www.edudel.nic.in/samagrashiksha/content/TrainingforInServiceTeachersandHeadTeachers.pdf)
- The Samagra Shiksha scheme was continued from 1 April 2021 to 31 March 2026, so norms for the current period may differ. — [Samagra Shiksha framework/PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1742290)
- CBSE 2017 fee schedule: general capacity-building programmes at INR 600 per day, and subject-specific programmes at INR 750 per day. — [CBSE Circular 42 Annexure C 2017](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2017/42_Circular_AnxC_2017.pdf)
- CBSE 2019: skill-course training fee of INR 1,000 per teacher per day, and a one-day wellness CBP at INR 1,000 per participant. — [CBSE Notification 118/2019](https://www.cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Notifications/2019/118_Notification_2019.pdf); [CBSE Notification 50/2019](https://www.cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Notifications/2019/50_Notification_2019.pdf)
- A school-hosted 2022 two-day English programme charged INR 2,000 per teacher. — [CBSE Notification 60/2022](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Notifications/2022/60_Notification_2022.pdf)
- Several 2025-2026 CBSE capacity-building notices say there is no fee, for example the skill-education and AI programmes. This means schools may expect free training. — [CBSE Notification 113/2025](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Notifications/2025/113_Notification_2025.pdf); [Careers360 on CBSE skill-education CBPs](https://news.careers360.com/cbse-ncert-skill-education-teachers-organise-capacity-building-programmes-january-5-classes-6-8-kaushal-bodh-event-up-delhi-wb-hr/amp)
- Trainer salaries (AmbitionBox, crowd-sourced): EdTech Academic Trainer INR 2.1-7 lakh/yr (1-13 years' experience); EdTech Robotics Trainer INR 3-6 lakh/yr (18 salaries) or INR 1-5.4 lakh/yr on another page. — [AmbitionBox Academic Trainer (EdTech)](https://ambitionbox.com/profile/academic-trainer-salary/edtech-industry); [AmbitionBox Robotics Trainer (EdTech)](https://ambitionbox.com/profile/robotics-trainer-salary/edtech-industry)

### Inferences
- **Estimate: trainer day cost.** A trainer on INR 3-6 lakh/yr over about 220 working days costs about INR 1,400-2,700 per day in salary. Add 20-30% for benefits and overhead, giving about INR 1,800-3,500 per day. A freelance master trainer is likely to cost INR 3,000-10,000 per day (assumption; no source found).
- **Estimate: travel and per diem** for tier-2 school visits is about INR 1,500-5,000 per trainer-day (assumption; no source).
- **Estimate: in-person orientation for 20-30 teachers.** One trainer-day plus travel plus printed materials is about INR 5,000-15,000 per session, or about INR 200-750 per teacher. This is comparable to the Samagra INR 315 per day norm and below CBSE's historic INR 600-1,000 fee.
- **Estimate: online session** (1-2 hours, up to 100 teachers). Trainer time plus a video licence costs roughly INR 2,000-5,000 per session, or under INR 100 per teacher.
- **Estimate: per school per year.** One orientation plus 2-4 online follow-ups plus a handholding visit comes to roughly INR 8,000-25,000 per school.

### Gaps
- No current (2024-26) Samagra Shiksha PAB norm per teacher per day was confirmed.
- No SCERT budgets were checked.
- No private-trainer day-rate survey or edtech cost-per-teacher-trained figure was found.

## Field sales and support: salaries and schools covered per person

### Takeaway
School sales executives in Indian education companies earn about INR 2.2-6 lakh/yr. Senior roles at larger firms reach INR 8-10 lakh. Academic trainers and counsellors earn INR 2-7 lakh/yr. No source was found for schools covered per person.

### Cited Findings
- National average sales executive salary is about INR 2.2 LPA (Naukri citing AmbitionBox). — [Naukri blog](https://www.naukri.com/blog/sales-executive-salary-in-india/)
- Education-company sales executive salaries (AmbitionBox; several marked low confidence): LEAD School INR 3.6-4.6 lakh; GUS Education INR 2.2-6 lakh (1-3 years); MASAI School INR 5.5-6.5 lakh; 21K School INR 4.7-6 lakh; Zee School INR 8.1-10.4 lakh (low confidence). — [AmbitionBox LEAD School](https://ambitionbox.com/salaries/lead-school-salaries/sales-executive); [AmbitionBox MASAI](https://ambitionbox.com/salaries/masai-school-salaries/sales-executive); [AmbitionBox 21K School](https://ambitionbox.com/salaries/21k-school-salaries/sales-executive); [AmbitionBox Zee School](https://ambitionbox.com/salaries/zee-school-salaries/sales-executive)
- Extramarks senior sales executive salary page exists on AmbitionBox (figures not extracted). — [AmbitionBox Extramarks](https://www.ambitionbox.com/salaries/extramarks-education-pvt-salaries/senior-sales-executive)
- Academic/robotics trainer salaries of INR 1-7 lakh/yr are given in the training section above. — [AmbitionBox Academic Trainer (EdTech)](https://ambitionbox.com/profile/academic-trainer-salary/edtech-industry)

### Inferences
- **Estimate: loaded cost per field sales executive** (salary, about 20% incentives and benefits, travel allowance INR 5,000-15,000 per month) is about INR 4-9 lakh/yr in tier-2 cities and INR 6-12 lakh/yr in tier-1 cities.
- **Estimate: schools covered** (assumption, not sourced). A field executive making 2-4 school visits a day might handle 80-150 prospect schools a year and convert perhaps 10-30. A trainer or academic support person might support 25-50 active schools. Validate these with industry interviews.

### Gaps
- No source on schools covered per sales person or per trainer per year in Indian K-12 publishing or edtech.
- City-tier salary split was not found.

## Online platform: MVP build cost, team salaries, cloud hosting, compliance, app stores

### Takeaway
Indian agency MVP quotes for education apps run about USD 20,000-70,000, roughly INR 18-62 lakh (estimate). A lean in-house team costs about INR 2-5 lakh per month (estimate from AmbitionBox salaries). Cloud hosting in AWS Mumbai is cheap at this scale: a t3.medium is about USD 0.0448 per hour, or about INR 2,900 per month. Inferred total hosting for 10k-100k students is about INR 15,000-1.5 lakh per month. CERT-In-empanelled mobile VAPT costs about INR 50,000-3 lakh per platform. DPDP compliance for a small firm runs from under INR 1 lakh to several lakh (sources conflict widely). App stores: Google Play USD 25 one-time; Apple USD 99 per year.

### Cited Findings
**Build cost (vendor blogs; commercial interest)**
- Indian vendor estimate: an EdTech platform costs INR 1.5-8.5 lakh, depending on LMS complexity. This likely refers to template or LMS builds rather than a custom block-coding app. — [ColorWhistle India](https://colorwhistle.com/website-cost-features/edtech-platform-development-cost-india/)
- MVP estimates for India-built education apps: USD 20,000-25,000 (basic/MVP); USD 25,000 for an India MVP over 3+ months versus USD 200,000 in the US; USD 20,000 at about USD 25 per hour. — [Solguruz](https://solguruz.com/blog/cost-of-developing-education-app/); [Ailoitte](https://www.ailoitte.com/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-make-an-educational-app/); [RipenApps](https://ripenapps.com/blog/?p=4856)
- Another guide: basic MVP USD 30,000-70,000, standard MVP USD 70,000-110,000. — [Debut Infotech](https://www.debutinfotech.com/blog/education-app-development-cost)
- Indian developer agency rates are commonly USD 15-40 per hour. Maintenance is often estimated at 15-20% of build cost per year. — [Apptunix](https://www.apptunix.com/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-build-an-educational-app/)

**Developer salaries (AmbitionBox, crowd-sourced)**
- React Native developer: about INR 1.5-11.5 lakh/yr on about 5,000 salaries (headline band INR 3.5-15 lakh). Infosys INR 5.2-12.4 lakh (2-6 years); EPAM INR 10-36 lakh (4-5 years). — [AmbitionBox React Native Developer](https://ambitionbox.com/profile/react-native-developer-salary)

**Cloud hosting (India region)**
- AWS EC2 t3.medium in ap-south-1 (Mumbai): about USD 0.0448 per hour on-demand (about USD 32.70 per month); lowest observed spot about USD 0.0194 per hour. Aggregator data; confirm on the AWS pricing page. — [DoiT compute ap-south-1 t3.medium](https://www.doit.com/compute/spot/ap-south-1/t3.medium); [aws-pricing.com](https://aws-pricing.com/t3.medium.html)

**Security audit / CERT-In**
- Mobile app VAPT ranges: INR 50,000-1.5 lakh per platform plus GST (2026 vendor guide); INR 1-3 lakh per platform for 5-10 days (CERT-In-empanelled firm); INR 70,000-1.8 lakh for one platform (3-5 person-days). A broad band of INR 40,000-8.5 lakh is also quoted. — [TCSA VAPT cost India 2026](https://www.tcsa.in/resources/vapt-cost-india-2026); [Qualysec CERT-In VAPT cost](https://qualysec.com/cert-in-vapt-audit-cost-india/); [CyberSigma VAPT cost](https://cybersigmacs.com/blog/vapt-cost-india/); [CyberNX](https://www.cybernx.com/vapt-testing-cost-guide)
- Each web app, API and mobile app is scoped and priced separately. CERT-In publishes the empanelled-auditor list and does not "certify" apps. — [CyberSigma empanelled vs non-empanelled](https://www.cybersigmacs.com/blog/cert-in-empanelled-vs-non-empanelled-auditor/)

**DPDP**
- DPDP Rules were notified in November 2025, and startups must prepare for the transition. — [YourStory, Nov 2025](https://yourstory.com/2025/11/with-data-protection-rules-notified-startups-need-to-prepare-for-a-pivotal-transition)
- Compliance deadline cited as 13 May 2027. A blog claims a startup with under 10,000 users can be substantively compliant for under INR 50,000 a year, while vendors quote INR 15 lakh-2 crore (no clear methodology). — [eCorpIT / DEV Community](https://ecorpit.com/dpdp-compliance-cost-indian-startups-2027-deadline/)
- Large-company estimates (legal fees INR 20-75 lakh) are not applicable to a startup. — [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/the-cost-of-consent-dpdp-may-add-up-to-rs-20-crore-to-company-budgets-150019.html)
- Cost items beyond legal fees include data mapping, vendor contract audits, a compliance officer, encryption and breach detection. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/dpdp-act-compliance-indian-startups-moving-at-different-speeds/)

**App store fees**
- Google Play: USD 25 one-time registration per developer account. Apple Developer Program: USD 99 per year (Enterprise USD 299). INR amounts depend on the exchange rate and GST. — [SplitMetrics, Aug 2025](https://splitmetrics.com/blog/google-play-apple-app-store-fees/); [Afkar Software 2026](https://afkarsoftware.com/en/blog-detail/google-play-console-account-2026-one-time-25-fee/)

### Inferences
- **Estimate: agency MVP.** An Android plus web build with Blockly, a teacher dashboard and offline sync is more complex than a basic content app. Expect the mid-to-upper part of the quoted ranges: roughly USD 30,000-70,000, or INR 26-62 lakh, over 4-8 months.
- **Estimate: in-house team** (salaries for 2 full-stack/React Native developers at INR 6-12 lakh each, 1 backend developer at INR 8-15 lakh, a part-time designer and QA at INR 4-8 lakh). This is about INR 25-50 lakh/yr, or INR 2-4 lakh per month. A 6-9-month build costs about INR 15-35 lakh. Loading for benefits, laptops and tools adds 15-25%.
- **Estimate: ongoing maintenance** at 15-20% of build per year is about INR 4-12 lakh per year (agency), or 1-2 retained developers in-house.
- **Estimate: cloud for 10,000 students** (low concurrency, offline-first sync, small JSON payloads): 2-3 small instances, a managed database, object storage and CDN cost roughly INR 15,000-40,000 per month. **For 100,000 students:** INR 60,000-1.5 lakh per month. These figures are derived from the t3.medium price of about INR 2,900 per month at INR 88/USD. Database, CDN and egress were not priced from primary sources.
- **Estimate: compliance, one-time.** VAPT for Android plus web plus API is about INR 1.5-6 lakh, with a retest included or about INR 0.5-1 lakh extra. A DPDP legal review (privacy notice, verifiable parental consent flow for children's data, data processing agreements with schools) is about INR 1-5 lakh one-time from a boutique firm, plus about INR 0.5-2 lakh per year. These are assumptions bracketed between the low blog estimate and the vendor quotes.
- **Estimate: app stores.** Google Play USD 25 is about INR 2,200 once. Apple USD 99 is about INR 8,700 per year, only if iOS is added.

### Gaps
- Pricing for AWS RDS, CloudFront and S3 in Mumbai, and for Azure Central India or GCP Mumbai, was not fetched from primary pages.
- No sourced estimate exists for concurrent-user load in school usage.
- No fixed-fee DPDP legal review quote from an Indian law firm was found. The DPDP children's-data (verifiable parental consent) cost implications were not costed from a source.
- Official INR prices for Apple and Google developer fees were not confirmed.

## Licensing of tools: Blockly, Scratch, Teachable Machine

### Takeaway
Blockly is Apache 2.0: free for commercial use, keep the notices. Scratch 3.0 code (scratch-gui and related repositories) is BSD-3-Clause, but MIT's trademark policy bars using "Scratch", the logo, Scratch Cat, Gobo and similar marks to endorse or promote derived products without written permission. Teachable Machine's open-source repositories are Apache 2.0. There are no licence fees, but a Scratch-derived editor must be rebranded.

### Cited Findings
- Blockly is free and open-source under the Apache License 2.0 (npm package licence Apache-2.0). — [npm blockly](https://npmjs.com/package/blockly); [Wikipedia: Blockly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blockly)
- A Wikipedia reference titled "Blockly graduates from Google" (28 Oct 2025) indicates a governance change. The fragment says Blockly remains under Apache 2.0. Details were not verified. — [Wikipedia: Blockly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blockly)
- Scratch 3.0 codebase licences are listed as BSD 3-Clause, GPLv2 and the Scratch Source Code License (older components). — [Wikipedia: Scratch](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/.sb2_file)
- The scratch-gui TRADEMARK file says MIT owns the Scratch name, logo, Scratch Cat and the Gobo, Pico, Nano, Tera and Giga graphics (the "Marks"), and that the Marks "may not be used to endorse or promote products derived from this software without specific prior written permission." — [scratch-gui TRADEMARK](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scratchfoundation/scratch-gui/develop/TRADEMARK)
- Teachable Machine repositories (teachable-machine-v1 and teachablemachine-community) are listed as Apache-2.0. — [ecosyste.ms: teachablemachine-community](https://awesome.ecosyste.ms/projects/github.com%2Fgooglecreativelab%2Fteachablemachine-community); [ecosyste.ms: teachable-machine-v1](https://awesome.ecosyste.ms/projects/github.com%2Fgooglecreativelab%2Fteachable-machine-v1)
- Apache 2.0 permits commercial use, provided copyright and licence notices are kept. — [Apache License summary](https://freelicence.azurewebsites.net/licenses/apache)

### Inferences
- Licence cost for all three tools is zero.
- Budget a small legal check of about INR 25,000-75,000 (assumption) to confirm attribution, NOTICE files and trademark-safe naming.
- Workbooks may refer to students using the free Scratch website, which is nominative use. A Scratch-based in-app editor would need its own name and artwork (no Scratch Cat).
- Using the hosted Teachable Machine website in lessons costs nothing. Embedding its models or code in the CodeSchool app relies on the Apache 2.0 repositories.

### Gaps
- The terms of service for the hosted Teachable Machine website, and the ownership terms for trained models, were not checked.
- Details of the Blockly governance change after Google (new steward, roadmap) were not confirmed.
- The Scratch Foundation's separate published trademark guidelines (beyond the GitHub file) were not fetched.
