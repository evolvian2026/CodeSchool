# Production costs for CodeSchool Offline Programme materials (India, 2025-2026)

Research date: 10 November 2026. All prices in INR. Most price data comes from trade-press price trackers, vendor price pages and IndiaMART listings, not audited quotes. No Indian printer publishes a formal per-forme rate card online, so the per-copy workbook figures below are built bottom-up (see Inferences) and should be checked against 2-3 real quotes from Delhi/Noida/Sivakasi printers.

## 1. Offset and digital printing cost per workbook copy (runs of 1,000 / 5,000 / 20,000 / 50,000; pilot of 100-1,000)

### Takeaway
I found no published Indian per-copy quote for a 96-128 page A4 4-colour workbook. Paper is the largest cost line. Maplitho paper cost Rs 53-82/kg in Aug-Sept 2026 and coated art paper Rs 88-157/kg. A bottom-up estimate puts a 112-page A4 4-colour workbook on 70 gsm maplitho at about Rs 35-50 per copy at 20,000-50,000 copies, about Rs 45-65 at 5,000 and about Rs 60-90 at 1,000. 2-colour saves roughly Rs 4-8 per copy. Digital colour at document-printing rates (Rs 4-8 per A4 colour page) would cost Rs 450-900 per copy, more than the MRP. A pilot should therefore be a 1,000-copy offset run, or digital in black-and-white or 2-colour, not a full-colour digital run.

### Cited Findings
**Paper prices (ex-godown, excl. GST, indicative)**
- 24 Aug-21 Sept 2026 tracker, unchanged since the 23 July 2026 update. Maplitho: Aaditiya Aswin Rs 53/kg, Shree Krishna Rs 54, Diblan Rs 64, Haryana Paper Converting Rs 63, R D Impex (Delhi) Rs 67, West Coast (Meenu Paper Mart, TN) Rs 75, Bahubali Rs 76, Sambhav Rs 76, Maheshwari Rs 77, Shree Ashtavinayak Rs 78, Jiwanmal (WB) Rs 78, KP Packaging Rs 82-97 — [Indian Printer & Publisher, paper and board prices, 15th update, 24 Aug / 21 Sept 2026](https://indianprinterpublisher.com/?p=99469)
- Same tracker, coated (gloss) art paper: JK Paper Rs 88-114/kg, West Coast Rs 94-127, BILT Rs 98-120, ITC PSPD Rs 103-128, imported China/Korea Rs 110-146, Mondi/APP imported Rs 125-157 — [Indian Printer & Publisher, Aug/Sept 2026](https://indianprinterpublisher.com/?p=99469)
- Same tracker, SBS board (cover stock): Rs 88-96/kg (JMV Rs 88, Kunj Impex Rs 94, RTC Rs 94, V5 Rs 96, and others). Grey-back duplex board: Rs 40-50/kg — [Indian Printer & Publisher, Aug/Sept 2026](https://indianprinterpublisher.com/?p=99469)

**Printing and binding rates (old benchmark only)**
- July 2011 NCERT job rates: Rs 40-45 per 1,000 sheets per side, one colour (23x36 in and 28x40 in sheets), and Rs 44 per 1,000 sheets (23x33 in). Printers said the market rate was "about double". NCERT binding rate was Rs 40 per 1,000 signatures. One printer (Anand Bros) cited a production cost of Rs 62,000 per lakh books, i.e. Rs 0.62 per book for print/bind job work only, excluding paper. These are 15-year-old, below-market government rates and are useful only as a structural guide — [PrintWeek India, 20 July 2011](https://printweek.in/news/book-printers-look-deal-16114)

**Whole-book benchmarks**
- Bulk book printing in India: 1,000 copies for about Rs 50,000-60,000 (Rs 50-60 per book). The source does not give spec, colour or binding — [OrangeBooks self-publishing cost guide, via search](https://www.orangebooks.in/blog/self-publishing-cost-india-complete-guide)
- IndiaMART listings vary widely and specs are mostly unclear: "Book Printing For School" A4, perfect-bound, black-and-white inside at Rs 6/piece; school stationery book printing at Rs 30/piece; 200-page A4 staple-bound homework notebook at Rs 85/piece — [IndiaMART text book printing services](https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/text-book-printing-services.html); [IndiaMART book printing](https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/book-printing.html)
- US benchmark for the colour premium (not India): at 1,000 copies of 200 pages, 2-colour costs about $3.50-5.50 per copy against $6-9 for full CMYK — [XW Print](https://www.xwprint.com/info/how-much-does-it-cost-to-print-a-textbook-a-r-103484628.html)

**Digital short-run**
- Digital colour A4 printing: Rs 6-20 per page at local shops, Rs 6-12 online for small orders, Rs 4-8 per page for 100-300 pages (mid-2026, indicative) — [Printster colour printing cost guide](https://printster.in/print/blog/colour-printing-cost-per-page-india)
- A Delhi-area printer says digital is cheaper and faster below about 500 copies because there is no plate cost — [SA Creative digital printing](https://sacreativestore.lovable.app/services/digital-printing)
- Industry view: short-run means 50-1,000 copies, where offset is inefficient. The per-copy cost of digital is "significantly higher" — [PrintWeek India, short-run digital book printing](https://www.printweek.in/features/best-practices-for-short-run-book-printing-in-digital-60976?amp=1)
- Printo's estimator lists colour A3 100 gsm prints at roughly Rs 15-24 per print, depending on quantity band — [Printo estimator](https://bzdel.estimator.printo.in/estimator/temphub.htm)

### Inferences
- **Paper per copy.** An A4 page is 0.0624 m². I allow about 15% for sheet trim and makeready waste.
  - 96 pp, 70 gsm: about 0.24 kg, Rs 15-19 at Rs 63-78/kg.
  - 112 pp, 70 gsm: about 0.28 kg, Rs 18-22.
  - 128 pp, 80 gsm: about 0.37 kg, Rs 23-29.
  - Coated art paper instead of maplitho (Rs 88-128/kg) would add roughly 40-70% to the paper line.
  - Cover: 300 gsm board, about 0.16 m² with waste, about 48 g, Rs 4-5. Add lamination, roughly Rs 1-2 (not sourced).
- **Impressions per copy.** A 23x36 in sheet folds to about 9x11.5 in, close to 8.5x11 trimmed, giving 16 pages per sheet (8 per side). A 112-page book is 7 sheets per copy.
  - 4-colour is 56 colour-impressions per copy. 2-colour is 28.
  - Take the 2011 market rate (about Rs 80-90 per 1,000 sheet-impressions per colour) and roughly double it for 2026 inflation. At Rs 0.15-0.20 per colour-impression, printing is about Rs 8-11 per copy for 4-colour and Rs 4-6 for 2-colour at long runs. The 2026 rate is my assumption, not sourced.
- **Plates and makeready.** These are fixed costs. On a 7-sheet, 4-colour job (56 plates), they matter at 1,000 copies and are negligible at 50,000. This is why the 1,000-copy cost is about double the 20,000+ cost.
- **Indicative per-copy totals** (112 pp, A4, 4C, 70 gsm maplitho, 300 gsm laminated cover, perfect bound):

  | Run | Per copy | Notes |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1,000 | Rs 60-90 | |
  | 5,000 | Rs 45-65 | |
  | 20,000 | Rs 38-52 | |
  | 50,000 | Rs 35-48 | |
  | 2-colour, any run | about Rs 4-8 less | |
  | Saddle-stitch, 96 pp | saves Rs 1-3 | Feasible at 96 pp on 70 gsm; marginal at 128 pp |

  These are model estimates and must be checked against quotes.
- **Classes 6-8 edition.** Two 24-32 page project booklets in self-cover or 170 gsm cover, saddle-stitched, add about Rs 6-12 per pair at 20,000+ copies and Rs 15-25 at 1,000.
- **Teacher script book.** About 150-200 pp in black-and-white or 2-colour, at section-level volumes of a few hundred to a few thousand. Expect roughly Rs 60-120 at offset, or Rs 150-300 printed digitally in black-and-white. Not sourced; estimate only.
- **Pilot.** Full-colour digital at Rs 4-8 per side would make a 112-page workbook cost Rs 450-900, which does not work against the Rs 449 MRP. For 100-500 pilot copies, the realistic options are:
  - Black-and-white or 2-colour digital (production presses quote far below document-shop rates; get a quote).
  - A minimum 1,000-copy offset run.

### Gaps
- I found no current (2025-26) Indian per-forme, per-plate (CTP) or makeready rate published online. These must come from direct quotes.
- No current per-copy binding or lamination rates, beyond the 2011 NCERT binding rate.
- No production digital-press book quote (e.g. HP Indigo or inkjet book press) for 100-1,000 copies of a colour workbook.

## 2. Production cost as a share of MRP for Indian school textbooks/workbooks

### Takeaway
No source gives a clean "production cost = X% of MRP" figure for Indian school books. The 15-25% rule of thumb could not be confirmed. Two related figures were found:
- Paper is about 60% of a school-book publisher's costs (2022).
- A publisher's net realisation is about 15-30% of MRP after trade margins.

On CodeSchool's numbers, a Rs 35-65 workbook is about 8-14% of the Rs 449 MRP. Adding booklets for Classes 6-8 keeps the total at about 7-12% of Rs 599.

### Cited Findings
- The Association of School Book Publishers (200+ members) said paper is about 60% of members' costs, when paper rose from Rs 80/kg to Rs 110/kg in 2022 — [Frontlist](https://frontlist.in/public/school-book-publishers-struggling-due-to-the-skyrocketing-increase-in-input-costs)
- A distribution-margin guide (vendor, not independent research) puts the publisher's share at 15-30% of MRP after distributor and retail margins. Printing, marketing and royalty are paid out of that share. Its example is a Rs 300 book with a 50% distributor discount, leaving Rs 150 net to the publisher — [IIP Books](https://iipbooks.com/blogs/view/distributor-margins-in-india-how-book-distribution-really-works)
- In 2011, textbooks, guides and workbooks cost a private-school student at most Rs 800-1,000 a year, and NCERT books cost Rs 15-130 — [PrintWeek India, 2011](https://printweek.in/news/book-printers-look-deal-16114)

### Inferences
- If paper is about 60% of cost, then at Rs 18-22 of paper per 112-page copy the total manufacturing cost is about Rs 30-37. That is consistent with the bottom-up Rs 35-48 at long runs.
- The commonly cited 15-25% may include pre-press, design, royalties, freight and wastage, not just print.

### Gaps
- No Indian publisher financials or interviews confirming a production-cost share of MRP for workbooks.

## 3. Kit components: 300-350 gsm cards, die-cut cut-outs, floor mat or banner, corrugated box, packing

### Takeaway
Only listing-level prices were found.
- Printed 300-350 gsm card sets: about Rs 120 per retail flash-card set on IndiaMART.
- Die-cutting: about Rs 1.2 per shape plus about Rs 275 setup (online printer).
- Star flex or PVC: Rs 12-40 per sq ft, so a 6x6 ft floor grid is roughly Rs 430-1,440.
- Corrugated board: about Rs 59-82/kg; kraft liner Rs 36-38/kg.

A per-section kit is plausibly Rs 800-2,000 in materials at small volume, with the mat and box the largest lines. This is an estimate.

### Cited Findings
- IndiaMART alphabet and number flash cards, 300 gsm art board: Rs 120 per set. Die-cut card listings range from Rs 10 to Rs 100 per piece — [IndiaMART flash card](https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/flash-card.html); [IndiaMART die-cut note card](https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/die-cut-note-card.html)
- Online-printer estimator: die cutting at Rs 1.21 per shape plus Rs 275 die setup. 350 gsm A3 colour prints cost about Rs 35-46 per print depending on quantity — [Printo estimator](https://bztel.estimator.printo.in/estimator/temphub.htm)
- Flex and banner rates:
  - Standard frontlit flex (280 gsm) Rs 12-18 per sq ft; star flex or reinforced PVC Rs 20-28; backlit Rs 22-32 (Delhi NCR, 2026 guide) — [Rajesh Company blog](https://rajeshcompany.pages.dev/blog/exhibition-banner-printing-delhi-ncr.html)
  - Indicative range Rs 12-40 per sq ft — [SA Creative](https://sacreativestore.lovable.app/services/flex-printing-signage)
  - Chennai listings: PVC star flex Rs 15 per sq ft; laminated eco-solvent vinyl about Rs 40 per sq ft — [IndiaMART Print Bazaar Chennai](https://m.indiamart.com/print-bazaar-chennai/printing-services.html)
  - Flex media alone Rs 18-45 per sq ft — [Sharda Associates project report](https://shardaassociates.in/pr-samples/flex-printing/)
- Corrugated inputs (Aug/Sept 2026):
  - 12 BF finished liner: Rs 35-38/kg (Silverton, NRAIL, ITC PSPD and others); export grade Rs 43-47 — [Indian Printer & Publisher](https://indianprinterpublisher.com/?p=99469)
  - Corrugated paperboard: Rs 59-82/kg (JB Craft Rs 59, Camerich Rs 60, Skyline and Spento Rs 63, Croplus/Cronza Rs 67-82) — [Indian Printer & Publisher](https://indianprinterpublisher.com/?p=99469)
  - Duplex board for a printed rigid or folding box: Rs 40-50/kg — [Indian Printer & Publisher](https://indianprinterpublisher.com/?p=99469)

### Inferences
- **Floor mat.** A 6x6 ft (36 sq ft) mat in star flex costs about Rs 430-1,000. An 8x8 ft (64 sq ft) mat costs about Rs 770-1,800. Laminated vinyl at about Rs 40 per sq ft gives Rs 1,440 (6x6) to Rs 2,560 (8x8). For durability on a classroom floor, a heavier PVC or laminated mat is safer, so budget at the top of the range.
- **Cards.** 350 gsm A3 colour sheets at Rs 35-46 each (digital, small volume), with about 8-16 cards per A3 sheet, give about Rs 3-6 per card. Offset on 350 gsm board at 1,000+ kits would cut this by more than half.
- **Kit box.** A printed 3-ply or 5-ply corrugated box of about 0.4-0.8 kg costs about Rs 25-60 in board, or about Rs 40-120 converted and printed. Inferred from Rs/kg; no box quote was found.

### Gaps
- No direct quote for cipher wheels (two-layer die-cut discs with a rivet), cut-out coding blocks, or a finished printed corrugated kit box.
- No Indian kitting or assembly labour rates.

## 4. Freight, logistics and warehousing within India

### Takeaway
Only parcel-level and aggregator data were found.
- A 1 kg surface parcel costs about Rs 50 (intra-city) to Rs 110 (rest of India).
- A 5 kg parcel costs about Rs 194 (intra-city) to Rs 632 (J&K/NE).
- B2B part-load adds 10-20% fuel surcharge and 10-80% zone/ODA premiums.
- Billing uses the higher of actual and volumetric weight (L x W x H cm / 5000).
- GST on freight is 5%.

A 112-page A4 workbook weighs about 0.3 kg, so a 30-book carton is about 9-10 kg.

### Cited Findings
- Delhivery indicative surface rates (aggregator calculator): 1 kg about Rs 50 intra-city, Rs 64 (next band), Rs 83 metro-to-metro, Rs 110 rest of India, Rs 168 J&K/NE. 5 kg about Rs 194 intra-city to Rs 632 J&K/NE. These are consumer-parcel rates, not negotiated B2B contracts — [ClickPost Delhivery rate calculator](https://www.clickpost.ai/delhivery-shipping-rate-calculator)
- iCarry B2B guide (14 May 2026):
  - Volumetric divisor 5000. Fuel surcharge 10-20% of base. Surface zone premiums: Zone B 10-20%, Zone C 20-35%, Zone D 40-80%. GST 5% on freight.
  - Part-load suits 50-5,000 kg; FTL suits above 5,000 kg.
  - Surface is 30-50% cheaper than air.
  - Worked example: a 10 kg carton Mumbai to Jaipur at Rs 150 base.
  — [iCarry](https://www.icarry.in/pages/blog/b2b-shipping-india.html)
- Delhivery B2B rate calculator requires a login on Delhivery One — [Delhivery help](https://help.delhivery.com/docs/b2b-serviceability-rate-calculator.md)
- One aggregator lists Gati surface express at Rs 90-140/kg metro-to-metro and Rs 150-280/kg national, with a 20 kg minimum. This looks high for surface cargo, so treat it as suspect — [ClickPost Gati](https://www.clickpost.ai/blog/gati-courier-charges)

### Inferences
- **Per-book freight.** The iCarry example works out to about Rs 15/kg base and about Rs 20/kg all-in, or about Rs 5-7 per workbook to a Zone B city. Remote or ODA schools could be 1.5-2x that.
- **Kit boxes.** Kits are bulky and light, so they will be charged on volumetric weight.
- **Planning figure.** Budget about 2-4% of MRP for outbound freight, plus inbound printer-to-warehouse freight.

### Gaps
- No Indian per-pallet or per-sq-ft warehousing rates were found.
- No actual PTL (Rivigo, Safexpress, TCI) per-kg rate cards were found.

## 5. ISBN, GST on books, workbooks, exercise books, printing services and kits, and the effect of GST 2.0 (22 Sept 2025)

### Takeaway
After the 56th GST Council meeting (3 Sept 2025, effective 22 Sept 2025):
- Printed books (4901) stay nil.
- Exercise books and notebooks (4820) fell from 12% to nil.
- Maps and charts (4905) are nil.
- Uncoated paper (4802) is 18% unless it is used for exercise books or notebooks (nil).
- Printing of books where the printer supplies the paper is 5% (SAC 9989).
- Corrugated and paper boxes (4819) are 5%, but paperboard is 18%.
- Board games and playing cards (9504) and non-electronic toys and puzzles (9503) fell from 12% to 5%.

A workbook with questions and answer spaces is a "printed book" (4901, nil) under the Delhi High Court's 2019 Sonka ruling. The kit is most likely 5% (9504/9503, or 4819 for the box). An exempt book publisher cannot recover the 18% GST on paper or the 5% on printing as input tax credit, so these taxes become a cost.

### Cited Findings
- Notification 9/2025-CT(R), 17 Sept 2025: uncoated paper and paperboard (4802) at 18%, excluding paper for exercise books, graph books, lab notebooks and notebooks. Notification 10/2025-CT(R) exempts that end-use paper (nil). Sources say the rate moved from 12% to nil and is effective 22 Sept 2025 — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=993339); [Taxscan](https://www.taxscan.in/top-stories/cbic-issues-gst-rate-notification-new-gst-rates-effective-from-22-september-2025-1432884)
- An AAR on ruled paper sheets held that paper for other uses attracts 9% CGST plus 9% SGST (18%) — [TaxGuru, In re Mangalam Kallani HUF (AAR WB)](https://taxguru.in/?p=1080380)
- Rate list (secondary source, not verified against notifications by that page):
  - Printed books 4901/4902/4903/4905: nil.
  - Exercise books and notebooks 4820: nil, down from 12%. Registers and business forms under 4820 stay at 18%.
  - Printing of books with publisher's content and printer's paper (SAC 9989): 5% with ITC.
  - General commercial printing: 18%.
  - Cartons and boxes 4819: 5%, down from 12% for corrugated.
  - Greeting cards, calendars and advertising material: 18%.
  - Nil-rated notebook makers must reverse ITC under Section 17(2) and Rules 42/43.
  — [TaxGarden, GST on printing services 2026](https://taxgarden.in/blog/gst-on-printing-services-books-packaging-india-2026)
- CBIC Circular 11/11/2017-GST (20 Oct 2017): printing of books where the publisher supplies content and the printer supplies paper is a service (9989). Printing where only a logo or design is supplied is a supply of goods — [TaxGarden](https://taxgarden.in/blog/gst-on-printing-services-books-packaging-india-2026)
- Delhi High Court, Sonka Publication India Pvt Ltd v. Union of India, WP(C) 10022/2018 (reported 13 May 2019), set aside an AAR ruling. It held that work or practice books which pose questions for the child to answer and let teachers assess understanding are "printed books" (4901, exempt), not exercise books (4820) — [StudyCafe](https://studycafe.in/work-books-or-practice-books-printed-and-sold-are-exempt-from-gst-68776.html)
- Paper and board trade (Aug/Sept 2026): the 3 Sept 2025 revision abolished most 12% slabs, leaving an inverted structure for cartons (18% on paperboard, 5% on cartons). Industry bodies have sought correction because of the working-capital burden — [Indian Printer & Publisher](https://indianprinterpublisher.com/?p=99469)
- Board games, chess, carrom, ludo and playing cards (9504) went from 12% to 5%. Non-electronic toys and puzzles (9503) also went to 5%. Electronic toys stay at 18%. All from secondary sources; some outdated tables still show 12-18% — [Busy toys GST](https://busy.in/gst-rates/toys/); [ClearTax rate revamp list](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-rate-revamp-list-of-cheaper-and-costlier-items)
- GST on freight (GTA, surface): 5% — [iCarry](https://www.icarry.in/pages/blog/b2b-shipping-india.html)

### Inferences
- **Workbook classification.** If CodeSchool's level workbook is mostly questions, activities and answer spaces, it fits 4901 (nil) under Sonka. Since 22 Sept 2025 it is nil under 4820 as well, so classification risk on the workbook is largely gone. Project booklets and the teacher script book are also 4901 (nil).
- **Tax as cost.** Input GST on printing (5%, or 18% on paper if bought separately) is a sunk cost for nil-rated output. Having the printer supply the paper under a 5% printing service is likely cheaper in tax terms than buying paper at 18% and paying for job work. Confirm with a CA.
- **Kit classification.** Selling the kit as a "game" or "puzzle" under 9504/9503 at 5% is plausible. If the kit is sold bundled with the workbook, composite or mixed-supply rules could apply the highest rate in the bundle. Pricing the kit as a separate line item avoids that ambiguity.

### Gaps
- No primary source was retrieved in this pass confirming that ISBNs are free from RRRLF (isbn.gov.in). This is believed correct from background knowledge but was not verified here.
- The CBIC notification texts were not read directly (the WB GST presentation PDF was not machine-readable). The rates above rely on secondary tax portals.
- No ruling was found on whether an educational coding kit (cards, mat, wheels) falls under 9504, 9503 or 4911/4823.

## 6. Paper price trends 2024-2026

### Takeaway
Maplitho rose from about Rs 45-72/kg in mid-2025 to about Rs 70-80/kg in early-to-mid 2026, then held flat from July to Sept 2026 at about Rs 53-82/kg. That is well below the 2022 peak of about Rs 110/kg, which publishers cited. Mills pushed increases in May-Aug 2025 (Rs 1,500-3,150/MT) and again in March 2026 (Rs 2-6/kg). The trade press flagged energy, logistics and Middle East risks as two-way pressure into late 2026.

### Cited Findings
- 2022: school-book paper rose from Rs 80/kg to Rs 110/kg — [Frontlist](https://frontlist.in/public/school-book-publishers-struggling-due-to-the-skyrocketing-increase-in-input-costs)
- May-Aug 2025: JK Paper, West Coast Paper Mills and Emami Paper announced coordinated increases of Rs 1,500-3,150/MT. On 16 March 2026, finished paper "reportedly surged by INR 2-6 per kg", citing LPG shortages (not widely substantiated), higher waste-paper costs and geopolitics. European mills raised prices about 4-8% — [PrintWeek India, 16 March 2026](https://www.printweek.in/article/paper-prices-surge-in-india/4hvhqhynsrdk979d4dg1bmhs46)
- Maplitho tracker: June 2025 Rs 45-72/kg; Aug 2025 Rs 48-70; Dec 2025 Rs 72 (West Coast); Feb 2026 about Rs 70-80; 21 July 2026 Rs 73-75 (Maheshwari, Haryana, Sambhav); 24 Aug-21 Sept 2026 unchanged since July, with no change across 70 grades — [Indian Printer & Publisher June 2025](https://indianprinterpublisher.com/blog/2025/06/paper-and-board-prices); [Aug 2025](https://indianprinterpublisher.com/?p=94135); [Aug/Sept 2026](https://indianprinterpublisher.com/?p=99469)
- Paper mills (Khanna Paper, Century Pulp & Paper) issued advisories on possible base-price adjustments after the GST slab revision — [Pulp & Paper Times](https://thepulpandpapertimes.com/news/khanna-paper-century-pulp--paper-2329)

### Inferences
- A cost model using Rs 75-80/kg for maplitho and Rs 100-120/kg for art paper (2026 mid-market), with a ±10% sensitivity, is reasonable.
- School-book printing peaks in Jan-March before the April session. Booking paper and press time in Oct-Dec avoids seasonal premiums. This is general industry knowledge, not sourced here.

### Gaps
- No IPMA (Indian Paper Manufacturers Association) data or index was retrieved.
- No Oct-Nov 2026 price update was found; the latest is 21 Sept 2026.
