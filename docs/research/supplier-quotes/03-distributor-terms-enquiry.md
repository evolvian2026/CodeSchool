# Enquiry: distribution and bookseller terms

Send to 2-3 school-book **distributors** and 2-3 **booksellers** who already supply CBSE schools' booklists in your target city. Best done as a short meeting or call, using this as the agenda, then confirmed by email. Replace everything in [square brackets].

The aim is to find out the **real terms**: discount off MRP, credit days and returns. The costs report found these decide whether the packs make money (default trade terms are about 45-55% off MRP with 140-160 days' credit).

> **Ground rule:** CodeSchool does **not** pay commissions, gifts or "donations" to schools or school staff for booklist adoption. Several states and courts now treat this as illegal. If a distributor says commissions are expected, note it, do not agree, and move on.

**Subject:** Distribution of CBSE computational thinking workbooks, 2027-28 session

Bullseye Knowledge System Limited is launching **CodeSchool**, a series of computational thinking and coding workbooks with classroom activity kits for CBSE schools, Classes 3-8, aligned to CBSE's new CT&AI curriculum (2026-27).

We expect to supply about [number] schools in [city/region] for the 2027-28 session, and would like to discuss working with you. Could you share your usual terms on the points below?

### Questions

**Terms**
1. What **discount off MRP** do you need on school booklist titles like these? Is it different for new publishers, or by volume?
2. How much of that do you pass on to retail booksellers?
3. What **credit period** do you work on, and when do you actually pay in practice (school books sell January to April)?
4. **Returns:** do you work on firm orders or sale-or-return? Is there a cap on returns (for example 10% of supply), and by when must returns be made?
5. Minimum order per title, and how you handle re-orders in April-May.

**Coverage and service**
6. Which cities, and roughly how many CBSE schools' booklists do you supply? Do you supply school book shops, or deliver to schools directly?
7. Can you fulfil **per-school firm orders** (we tell you the school and section counts and you deliver against them), so that returns stay low?
8. Do you handle **specimen copies** to schools, or should the publisher do that?
9. What do you expect from the publisher in marketing support or school visits?

**Alternatives**
10. Would you act as an **appointed bookseller** for named schools on a lower discount (we are considering about 35% off MRP, no school commission), with the publisher doing the school marketing?
11. Do schools you work with buy material **centrally** (school pays, adds to fees)? If so, how do they pay (advance, on delivery, credit)?

**Paperwork**
12. Invoicing and GST documents you need from us (books are nil-rated; the kit may be invoiced separately at 5%).
13. Delivery terms: who pays freight to your godown and from there to schools or shops?

Thank you. A short call this week would be very helpful: [Phone] / [Email].

Regards,
Anoop Dobhal
CodeSchool, Bullseye Knowledge System Limited
