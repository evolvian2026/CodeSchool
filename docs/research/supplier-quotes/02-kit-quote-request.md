# Request for quotation: classroom activity kits

Send to 2-3 suppliers of printed educational games, flash cards or board games (often the same offset printers, or Sivakasi/Delhi card and game makers). Replace everything in [square brackets]. Ask for a **physical sample** before any order: the kit has to survive a full school year of use by 40 children.

**Subject:** Request for quotation – printed classroom activity kit (school use)

Bullseye Knowledge System Limited is developing **CodeSchool**, a computational thinking programme for CBSE schools (Classes 3-8). ### 2. Quantities

Please quote per kit at **50 / 200 / 1,000 / 3,000 kits**, and the add-on project pack at the same quantities.

- Price per kit and per component, so we can change components later.

- A **prototype kit** (price and delivery time) before the main order.

- **GST rate and HSN** you would apply (we understand printed educational games, puzzles and cards are generally 5% after September 2025; please confirm with your tax adviser).

- Payment terms and minimum order.
- Durability: recommended lamination or material for a year of daily classroom use.
